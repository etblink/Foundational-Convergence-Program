## 6. Highest-information structural findings

### Operational equivalence

Operational equivalence is a strong native organizing principle: preparations/processes can be identified by equality of outcome probabilities against all allowed tests. Its physical content remains relative to the allowed effect/test family and does not become gauge or ontological equivalence automatically.

### Measurement semantics

GPTOPT natively supplies laboratory-facing preparation/effect/transformation semantics, but not complete framework-wide calibration or one exhaustive physical effect algebra.

### Composite-system ambiguity

This is the strongest local-to-global result. Local state spaces do not uniquely determine the global composite theory. Even under Tomographic Locality, the source-bound GPT framework admits inequivalent composite state spaces between minimal and maximal tensor-product bounds.

### Dynamics

Native transformations are K3 possibility structure. Base GPTOPT supplies no universal K4 physical history selector.

### Causality

Nonsignalling, operational causality, local tomography and Lorentzian spacetime causality are distinct. PR-box/boxworld-type GPTs show that nonsignalling alone does not select quantum correlations.

## 7. Reconstruction result

The bounded corpus contains multiple selection mechanisms rather than one unique inevitable derivation:

- Hardy 2001: the continuity/reversible-transformation axiom does material quantum-selection work; dropping continuity yields the classical alternative in that reconstruction;
- CDP purification: purification is an added principle with strong conditional consequences, not a base theorem and not source-bound here as a unique selector of complex QM;
- Müller 2021 synthesis: Tomographic Locality + Continuous Reversibility + Subspace Axiom provide another reconstruction package.

FCP-7 therefore records:

`RECONSTRUCTION_SUCCESS != INDEPENDENT_PREDICTION`.

No bounded axiom package is established as uniquely minimal or uniquely selected by experiment.

## 8. Empirical result

The FCP-4 source window contained no direct GPT experimental source sufficient for K10-B. FCP-7 adds one minimal supplement:

`SRC-FCP7-GPT-MAZUREK-2021` — Mazurek, Pusey, Resch & Spekkens, *Experimentally Bounding Deviations From Quantum Theory in the Landscape of Generalized Probabilistic Theories*, PRX Quantum 2, 020302 (2021), DOI `10.1103/PRXQuantum.2.020302`, arXiv `1710.05948`.

This experiment performs GPT tomography without assuming the quantum formalism for states/measurements and constrains deviations from the quantum description for single-photon polarization.

FCP interpretation:

- positive evidence that experiments can constrain GPT theory space: **YES**;
- global empirical selection of quantum theory from all GPTOPT possibilities: **NO**;
- confirmation of the GPTOPT meta-framework itself: **NO**;
- empirical validation of one reconstruction axiom package: **NO**.
