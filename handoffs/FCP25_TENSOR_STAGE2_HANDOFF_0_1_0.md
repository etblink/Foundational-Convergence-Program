## 2. Why outcome D is selected

The exact frozen corpus establishes three broad, overlapping proposition clusters but no source-bound foundational successor:

- MPS, PEPS, DMRG, ER/MERA, TRG, TDVP and cMERA are representations, variational/computational methods, or RG architectures for supplied quantum/statistical/QFT objects. Their tensor structure does not independently supply ontology or dynamics.
- Holographic tensor networks and QEC constructions are rigorous model, code, dual-description and reconstruction structures. Their physical reading is principally conditioned on AdS/CFT, CFT code subspaces, RT-like targets, chosen graphs/codes, or toy/large-bond limits.
- Information-first geometry/gravity sources use materially different carriers, factorizations, backgrounds, target equations and reconstruction principles. The frozen scope does not establish a shared model class, equivalence doctrine, internal dynamics, calibration bridge, or persistent framework identity.

Outcome C is not selected merely because these clusters differ: no cluster independently meets the source-binding requirement for a stable foundational competitor. No placeholder successor ID is created. The scientific literature and all 29 source records remain preserved at their warranted levels.
