## 1. Adjudicated posture

The external audit survives independent review **materially, but not verbatim**.

The most important retained conclusions are:

1. **E3 equal-standard defect (W3).** FCP-11 permitted bounded CST E3 subclaims while FCP-16/20 applied a stricter packet-transcription ceiling to LOOP/AS. Under one common standard, bounded target-conditioned LOOP/AS E3 may become nonzero; independent strong/moderate convergence and independent framework-level E4 remain zero.

2. **E2 equal-standard defect (W8/W13).** Earlier AQFT/CQM E2 relations were admitted at a looser transcription burden than FCP-14. FCP-14’s no-E2 result remains scientifically defensible, but its provenance reason was partly defective. Reanalysis must cover FCP-5/FCP-13/FCP-14 under one E2 standard.

3. **No true NFC blinding (W2).** K1–K10 were frozen before pairwise scoring and are substantially framework-neutral in wording, but the comparison agenda was already conditioned by NFC material. Stop describing the freeze as blinded.

4. **Dual-firewall scope narrowing (W1).** The negative NFC comparisons are not logically tautological. They are structurally predisposed and method-conditioned. Their exact type-mismatch conclusions survive; nature-level absence interpretations do not.

5. **NFC provenance dependency (W4).** The frozen canonical NFC Git commit is not publicly retrievable, while strong bundle/archive bindings survive. The correct state is `PARTIALLY_REPRODUCIBLE`, not “no provenance.” Canonical source reproduction should precede another NFC comparison.

6. **Closed-corpus zeros survive with narrower interpretation (W5/W18).** FCP-18’s recurrence arithmetic remains valid under the original frozen method and corpus. It should not be elevated into a representative claim about what does or does not recur in nature, and its historical `GOVERNANCE_REVIEW_CANDIDATE=0` was too confident.

7. **Target-conditioned recovery needs two labels (W7/W11).** Recovery of supplied GR/QM targets is not independent discovery, but can be positive viability evidence. The composite null is a fair empirical incumbent and an asymmetric complete-foundation comparator.

8. **Targeted source strengthening is justified later (W6/W9/W10).** AS needs material criticism/physical-realization coverage; AQFT split/nuclearity requires a targeted FIS crosswalk; LQC may be bound as a LOOP extension only with a no-backprojection rule.
