## 2. Why the outcome is inconclusive

All eleven named candidates were uniquely identified. Ten exact candidate texts were sufficient for proposition-level comparison. The published 1984 Green–Schwarz anomaly-cancellation paper was not: the journal page was unavailable and an exact publisher reprint remained behind security verification. Abstract-level publisher metadata was not used to infer body-level assumptions, caveats, or redundancy.

The sole frozen comparator for that candidate is Agmon et al. 2023, a later review that explicitly states and cites the Green–Schwarz anomaly-cancellation result. That is evidence favoring lane-level coverage, but it does not independently establish that rejecting the original primary entails no loss of primary evidence. Candidate F007-CAND-01 therefore receives SOURCE_TEXT_INSUFFICIENT.

Under the frozen outcome vocabulary, one unresolved eligible primary-source comparison prevents a completed eleven-source redundancy verdict. REAUDIT_INCONCLUSIVE is conservative: it does not claim a material omission, and it does not clear Finding-007.
