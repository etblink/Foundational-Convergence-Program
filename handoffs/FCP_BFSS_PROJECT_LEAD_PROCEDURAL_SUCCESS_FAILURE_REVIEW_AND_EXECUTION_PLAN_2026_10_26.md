### Proposed bounded CI standard for future gates

1. **One-time dependency qualification:** On a *separate green* run, compile the exact frozen original S4B3–S4B33 chain and 27 concrete true Pauli/Fock parents; verify all exact Git source blobs, Lean/Mathlib/original math pins, complete source and `.olean` manifests, allowed axioms, and kernel import via a native Lean consumer. Save a new content-addressed combined parent checkpoint **only after positive strict acceptance**; a red candidate job's diagnostic artifacts are not automatically qualified ancestry.
2. **Fast leaf proof gate:** Fail closed unless this exact accepted checkpoint (or independently accepted equivalent pair of caches) restores with matching manifest and original source/toolchain identities. Freshly compile **only the candidate** `SU2BFSSPhysicalDomainS4B34ConcretePauliPairedManuscriptBProbe.lean`; validate unchanged exact theorem statement/root, nonempty new `.olean`, compiler exit 0 and `#print axioms` restricted to `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`; reject `sorryAx` and unsupported warnings. Never reuse an unqualified candidate blob.
3. **Full requalification on invalidation:** A dependency, pin, mathlib, source, closure or manifest change invalidates the cache and requires fresh parent build. Schedule full closure rebuilds at milestones independently of routine leaf iteration. Retain logs, manifest, witness consumers, and a dated acceptance audit.
4. **Proof method:** Use exact imported lemmas with opaque typed interfaces and *leaf-local* `Finset.sum_congr` or `sum_comm`, not unrestricted `simp_rw` under large original Fock terms. Cache optimization cannot itself fix Lean's kernel deterministic timeout; split the remaining mathematical proof into smaller kernel-checkable declarations and refuse to increase budgets without diagnostics.
