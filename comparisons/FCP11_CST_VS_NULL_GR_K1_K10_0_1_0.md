## 4. K1 — carrier: additional, not reformulation

GR's working carrier is continuum Lorentzian geometry with matter fields. CST instead takes locally finite causal sets as fundamental configurations, including causal sets with no manifold approximation.

No source-bound isomorphism, representation equivalence or controlled limit identifies the **full CST configuration space** with the GR solution/configuration space.

Therefore:

`K1 = ADDITIONAL_FOUNDATIONAL_COMMITMENT`.

The scientifically important addition is not generic partial-order mathematics by itself. It is the physical hypothesis that a locally finite causal order is fundamental spacetime structure.

This receives no empirical-selection upgrade merely because the null baseline has no UV-complete quantum-gravity carrier.

## 5. K2 — redundancy: generic functional analogy only

CST order isomorphism/relabeling redundancy and GR diffeomorphism redundancy both prevent arbitrary labels from becoming physical structure.

That broad role is real but insufficient for stronger correspondence:

- the carrier objects differ;
- the transformation/equivalence groups are not source-bound as the same structure;
- discrete general covariance is dynamics-specific rather than minimal C0 identity;
- quotienting/relabeling is common mathematics.

FCP-11 status:

`E5_FUNCTIONAL_ANALOGY` at most.

No strong or moderate convergence credit.

## 6. K3 — allowed transformations: extension-dependent

Minimal CST supplies no universal physical transformation/evolution rule. Order embeddings and relabellings are mathematical maps; sprinkling is a continuum-to-discrete sampling/realization procedure.

One-element growth becomes a physical-history candidate only after a CSG/QSG law is supplied.

The null baseline, by contrast, contains actual GR and QFT/SM sector dynamics plus their corresponding physical perturbations/interactions.

FCP-11 therefore does not manufacture a shared process category from vocabulary such as `growth`, `flow`, `map`, or `evolution`.

Primary status: `EXTENSION_DEPENDENT`.

## 9. K6 — causal structure and Lorentz compatibility

K6 contains one of CST's clearest non-null primitive commitments:

- causal order is fundamental/interpreted;
- local finiteness supplies fundamental discreteness;
- continuum Lorentzian geometry is a target rather than primitive input.

### 9.1 Faithful embedding relation

For a declared faithful/manifoldlike relation, the embedding preserves the intended order/causal correspondence and fixes number/volume statistically at the chosen density.

At this limited representation bridge, an explicitly source-bound embedding can receive bounded `E2_FUNCTORIAL_REPRESENTATION` status in the FCP sense of a structure-preserving representation map.

Its scope ceiling is strict:

- it is not an inverse reconstruction of every causal set;
- it does not establish unique continuum geometry;
- it does not show that CST dynamics selects the embedded causal set;
- it is target-directed rather than independent convergence.

### 9.2 Lorentz theorem

The BHS result demonstrates that a Poisson sprinkling in Minkowski spacetime does not intrinsically yield an equivariantly extractable preferred direction.

This is a real nontrivial compatibility achievement. But Minkowski geometry is supplied to the theorem. Therefore it is not a derivation of Lorentzian spacetime and not framework-level empirical selection.

- existence of fundamental discreteness: `ADDITIONAL_FOUNDATIONAL_COMMITMENT`;

## 11. K8 — globalization: reconstruction is not selection

CST's continuum/globalization burden is intrinsically stronger than the null baseline's ordinary geometric consistency burden because the framework must explain why the fundamental causal set belongs to a manifoldlike sector at all.

The source corpus contains nontrivial dimension/topology/geometric estimators and sharpened continuum-uniqueness results. These can support selected E3-level reconstruction statements.

But three logically distinct propositions remain:

1. a causal set is manifoldlike / faithfully embeddable;
2. the approximating continuum is appropriately unique;
3. the fundamental dynamics preferentially produces such causal sets.

The standard sprinkling construction directly realizes (1) only after a continuum target is chosen. FCP-11 therefore treats successful reconstruction on sprinkled/manifoldlike inputs as `TARGET_CONDITIONED_RECONSTRUCTION`.

The Kleitman–Rothschild witness blocks the inference that generic causal-set kinematics selects realistic spacetime.

Hauptvermutung status remains:

`TRADITIONAL_FINITE_CONTINUUM_UNIQUENESS_OPEN_REFINED`.
