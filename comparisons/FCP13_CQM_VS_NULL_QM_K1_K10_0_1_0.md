## 4. K1 — state-as-process is reformulation, not ontology

In a concrete quantum categorical model, a state `I -> A` can represent an ordinary quantum state and an effect `A -> I` an ordinary quantum effect. This is structurally meaningful and supports bounded E2 representation status.

However:

- the categorical shell also accommodates non-Hilbert/toy models;
- generic object/morphism syntax does not select the quantum state space;
- no deeper physical ontology is derived.

> **STATE-AS-MORPHISM PRESENTATION != NEW PHYSICAL STATE ONTOLOGY.**

Primary bucket: `REFORMULATION`.

## 5. K2 — categorical equality is not physical redundancy

Diagrammatic equality, coherence and equality of morphisms belong to the mathematical theory/presentation.

Ordinary quantum physical redundancy can involve basis, phase, gauge or representation equivalence depending on the model. No frozen source establishes identity between these doctrines and generic CQM coherence.

At most E5 functional analogy.

> **DIAGRAMMATIC EQUALITY != PHYSICAL GAUGE REDUNDANCY.**

## 6. K3 — process-first structure is the clearest CQM refinement

K3 is the strongest defining CQM key.

Concrete standard quantum processes can be organized as morphisms whose sequential composition reproduces ordinary composition and whose monoidal composition reproduces ordinary composite-system/tensor process structure at the declared model scope.

The generic categorical shell is not distinctive. What remains source-qualified is the systematic **process-first compositional abstraction** that treats states, effects, transformations and protocols uniformly as processes and supports model-independent diagrammatic reasoning under explicit categorical hypotheses.

This is best classified as `STRUCTURAL_REFINEMENT`, with bounded E2 relation to concrete ordinary quantum process models.

It does not independently select quantum theory because weaker classical/stochastic/toy process categories share the shell.

## 14. CQM-specific structural tests

### State-as-process

Bounded E2 representation in concrete quantum models; primary reformulation. No new ontology.

### Sequential/parallel composition

Generic shell: zero distinctive credit. Quantum process organization: structural refinement when concrete quantum semantics and hypotheses are explicit.

### Dagger

Optional. In quantum models it can represent adjoint structure and support dagger-specific theorems. It is not part of every CQM object and does not uniquely select quantum theory.

### Compactness

Optional. It supports map-state duality, cups/caps, entanglement/protocol topology and diagrammatic reasoning. Non-Hilbert compact categories block uniqueness; classify as extension-level structural residue, not empirical selection.

### Frobenius/classical structures

Optional. They provide a categorical treatment of classical data/observable-like structure within quantum processes. They are structural tools whose physical meaning depends on the selected model.

### CPM/mixed-state machinery

Optional. It provides a compositional construction corresponding to completely positive/mixed processes where supplied. It is best treated as reformulation/generalization of ordinary CP-map structure rather than new empirical physics.

### Graphical calculus

Generic string-diagram/coherence machinery gets zero physical credit. Source-qualified quantum-specialized diagrammatic theorems may count as structural refinement under their exact hypotheses, not as new empirical content.

### Toy-model witness

## 16. Material CQM/null differences

FCP-13 records five material scope differences:

1. **framework breadth:** generic CQM/process models include non-Hilbert/toy realizations beyond ordinary quantum theory;
2. **process-first abstraction:** CQM elevates compositional process structure as its organizing language rather than treating it as one feature of a Hilbert/QFT presentation;
3. **dynamics scope:** ordinary quantum models contain actual dynamics; generic CQM does not select one;
4. **locality/scale scope:** null QFT contains physical locality and RG/EFT structure absent from minimal CQM;
5. **calibration:** ordinary quantum/QFT models possess mature empirical calibration; CQM inherits it only through concrete realizations.

These are qualitative differences, not a score.
