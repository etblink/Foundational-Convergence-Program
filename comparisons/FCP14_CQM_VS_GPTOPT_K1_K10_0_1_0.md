## 4. K1 — state-as-process versus probabilistic state geometry

The generic CQM typing `state : I -> A` and `effect : A -> I` is not by itself a convex or probabilistic theory. In the categorical-probabilistic bridge, enriched state/effect processes can be related to operational states/effects with probability semantics, supporting a restricted E2 representation relation.

What is not preserved framework-wide:

- one unique convex state cone;
- one physical effect set;
- normalization/causality doctrine across all CQM;
- all GPTOPT models;
- physical realization.

GPTOPT retains a materially broader theory-space burden: classical, quantum and nonquantum/postquantum state spaces are admitted, and the physical state/effect set is model/empirically selected.

> **STATE-AS-PROCESS != CONVEX STATE-SPACE SELECTION.**

## 5. K2 — categorical equality versus operational equivalence

CQM equality/coherence is mathematical equality in the chosen categorical theory. GPTOPT operational equivalence identifies preparations/processes only relative to the admitted family of tests/effects, with separation/tomographic completeness itself a burden.

The frozen corpus does not establish both directions of an identification:

- categorical equality -> operational equivalence may hold in a selected semantics;
- operational indistinguishability -> categorical equality is not framework-wide source-bound.

Therefore the strongest framework relation is E5 functional analogy, with a material difference in equivalence doctrine.

> **CATEGORICAL EQUALITY != OPERATIONAL EQUIVALENCE.**

## 6. K3 — process skeleton and probability enrichment

The shared bare shell—systems/objects, processes/morphisms, sequential composition, parallel/monoidal composition—is supported by many weaker process theories and earns zero distinctive convergence credit.

The nontrivial bridge begins only when process composition is combined with explicit probabilistic/operational structure. At that restricted scope the comparison map is:

- system object -> operational system;
- state/effect process -> operational preparation/effect;
- process morphism -> operational transformation/event;
- sequential composition -> sequential circuit composition;
- monoidal composition -> declared parallel/composite composition;
- scalar/probability evaluation -> operational outcome probability where the bridge hypotheses supply it.

This is a restricted E2 bridge relation. The frozen corpus does not establish that it is full, faithful, essentially surjective or an equivalence of the entire `FW-CQM` and `FW-GPTOPT` model classes.

## 7. K4 — shared absence is not convergence

Both minimal frameworks organize possible transformations without one universal law selecting actual temporal history.

This is a scope similarity, not a positive convergence relation. When both instantiate ordinary quantum theory, the same unitary/CP dynamics can be represented, but that is `QUANTUM_INHERITED_OVERLAP`.

> **SHARED ABSENCE OF A HISTORY SELECTOR != CONVERGENCE.**

## 11. K8 — the decisive composite-system asymmetry

GPTOPT explicitly exposes this selection burden. For fixed local state spaces, source-bound models admit distinct minimal, maximal and intermediate composite constructions. Local tomography, where assumed, does not uniquely select the quantum composite cone.

The categorical-probabilistic bridge can preserve formal composition at restricted E2 scope, but this does not discharge the GPTOPT composite-selection burden.

Therefore K8 is primarily `MATERIAL_DIVERGENCE` even though a formal bridge relation exists.

> **MONOIDAL PRODUCT != UNIQUE PHYSICAL COMPOSITE CONE.**

> **LOCAL TOMOGRAPHY != CATEGORICAL COHERENCE.**
