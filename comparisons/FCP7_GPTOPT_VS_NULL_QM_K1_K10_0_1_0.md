## 2. K1 — carrier result

GPTOPT does not define one universal physical state space. It defines a language/class in which a concrete theory specifies its state spaces and effect/transformation structure. Quantum density matrices are one realization, not a generic base conclusion.

The framework-level relation to QM is therefore **generalization**, not independent convergence and not primarily reformulation.

## 3. K2 — operational equivalence result

Operational equivalence is a genuine native clarification: two preparation procedures can be represented by the same state when every allowed effect assigns the same outcome probabilities. Analogous equivalence can be applied to transformations.

However, the conclusion is relative to the admitted test/effect family. FCP-7 does not identify operational indistinguishability with gauge redundancy or a unique ontology.

## 4. K3/K4 — process versus dynamics

GPTOPT cleanly separates a space of allowed transformations from a law of actual temporal development. This is scientifically useful but does not solve K4.

The existence of reversible transformations, channels or process composition does not imply a Hamiltonian/history selector. Quantum dynamics are inherited only after the quantum model is selected.

## 5. K5 — measurement result

GPTOPT's strongest base-level physical semantics occur at K5. Preparations and measurements are tied directly to laboratory procedures at an abstract operational level, and effects encode outcome probabilities.

Nevertheless:

- the physically available effect set is model data;
- no-restriction is not framework-wide;
- abstract effects are not automatically calibrated detector hardware;
- completeness/exhaustiveness is not established by operational naming alone.

## 6. K6 — causality result

FCP-7 preserves three distinct levels:

1. nonsignalling constraints on correlations;
2. operational causality/order assumptions;
3. Lorentzian spacetime causal structure.

The first two do not derive the third. Barrett's post-quantum examples show that nonsignalling alone permits stronger-than-quantum correlations, so it cannot be used as a quantum-selection theorem.

## 7. K7 — scale result

No source-bound framework-wide RG doctrine exists in the bounded GPTOPT corpus. Mixing, discarding and coarse-graining are operational/probabilistic operations, not a substitute for physical renormalization-group flow.

## 8. K8 — composition result

This is FCP-7's highest-information structural result.

Even when Tomographic Locality is imposed, local systems do not uniquely determine the allowed global state space. In the bounded modern GPT synthesis, a tomographically local composite can occupy a range between minimal and maximal tensor products. The quantum composite rule is therefore a selected subtheory structure, not a generic consequence of local operational data.

This provides a precise local-to-global selection burden without claiming geometric/spacetime globalization.

## 9. K9 — realization result

GPTOPT has genuine **operational semantics** but incomplete **physical calibration** at framework level.

A state can mean an equivalence class of preparations and an effect an equivalence class of measurement events while the theory still leaves open whether the realized system is a photon, spin, field mode, thermodynamic object or another system and how numerical physical units/couplings are fixed.
