# Foundational Convergence Program Charter

## 1. Mission

### Ultimate purpose

The ultimate purpose of the Foundational Convergence Program (FCP) is to contribute to the search for truth about physical reality: to determine, as far as rigorous reasoning, mathematics, observation, and experiment permit, what nature is actually like.

Foundational convergence is not itself the ultimate objective. It is one investigative strategy. Independently developed frameworks that are forced toward the same non-generic structures may identify especially valuable candidates for deeper mathematical and empirical investigation.

Failure to find convergence is also a valid result. It may show that proposed common structures are generic, lineage-dependent, target-conditioned, optional, empirically inherited, or absent at the current source scope.

No framework—including Reduced NFC, the null baseline, any other admitted framework, or FCP's own methodology—is entitled to preservation if stronger evidence points elsewhere.

### Operative comparative question

Within that broader truth-seeking purpose, FCP's central comparative question is:

> **Which structures recur independently across viable foundational theories, and which are genuinely forced by successful physics?**

The program is not organized around defending, replacing, or completing any preferred Theory of Everything. Its task is to distinguish results that survive across frameworks from structures that depend on a particular language, ontology, model choice, or realization map.

This clarification is prospective program-purpose governance. It does not reinterpret or modify the scientific conclusions of FCP-1 through FCP-21, nor does it alter the frozen FCP-2 comparison keys or equivalence/convergence rules.

## 2. Neutrality contract

> **No framework receives evidentiary credit merely because it suggested the question being tested.**

A framework that first motivates a question receives no presumption that a similar structure found elsewhere is evidence for that framework. Convergence credit requires independently source-bound structures with an explicit equivalence or comparison argument.

> **The null competitor — established GR + QFT + Standard Model without deeper ontology — is a first-class competitor.**

The null competitor must be allowed to win whenever deeper foundational machinery yields no explanatory, predictive, structural, or empirical gain under the common comparison protocol.

## 3. Governing scientific principle

> **Seek truth about reality.**
>
> **Preserve results, not theories.**

The second principle serves the first. The program may retain a theorem, countermodel, invariant, obstruction, empirical result, or useful formal architecture while rejecting the framework-level interpretation originally attached to it. Neither a theory nor the FCP methodology itself is protected from revision when stronger evidence or reasoning points elsewhere.

## 4. Scope

FCP may compare, when separately admitted and source-bound:

- the GR + QFT + Standard Model null competitor;
- reduced NFC;
- operational/algebraic quantum frameworks;
- causal-set or related order-theoretic approaches;
- loop/spinfoam or related background-independent discrete geometry;
- tensor-network and information-theoretic approaches;
- asymptotic safety;
- string-theoretic and holographic approaches;
- categorical, process-theoretic, or topos-style foundational approaches;
- additional frameworks admitted later under the same rules.

Admission is not endorsement. Listing is not evidence. A framework receives comparative credit only through separately authorized, source-bound analysis under the common protocol.

## 5. Core comparison burdens

Every framework must be examined under the same ten burdens:

1. primitive assumptions;
2. mathematical structures actually derived from them;
3. structures supplied by model choice;
4. physical-realization bridges;
5. dynamics;
6. observable definitions;
7. empirical predictions;
8. falsification conditions;
9. unresolved selection problems;
10. whether the same result follows in a weaker framework.

FCP-2 further freezes K1–K10 comparison coordinates, M1–M3 status metadata, and E1–E5 equivalence/convergence rules. Those versioned artifacts control later comparisons unless explicitly superseded by a governance revision.

## 6. Prohibited promotions

The program must not infer physical truth from:

- mathematical elegance;
- internal consistency;
- universal properties alone;
- canonicity relative to a supplied category, test language, or process universe;
- numerical coincidence;
- historical priority;
- popularity or authority;
- successful reproduction of already-known inputs;
- downstream success whose upstream bridge has not been proved.
