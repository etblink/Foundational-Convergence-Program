## 7. Counterevidence and assumption-sensitive tensions

### Causal nonseparability versus causal-inequality violation

The switch is causally nonseparable yet does not violate the ordinary causal inequalities in the foundational witness setting. Later extended-party constructions can certify indefinite order under additional relativistic/free-intervention assumptions, and 2026 experiments test corresponding Bell-like causal-order inequalities with remaining loopholes.

### Quantum realizability versus classical-spacetime realizability

Time-delocalized subsystem constructions provide standard-quantum realizations for substantial process classes, including causal-inequality-violating examples. Current top-down closed-lab work in a classical acyclic spacetime derives a narrower QC-QC class under explicit assumptions. These are distinct physicality questions rather than contradictory yes/no results.

### Dynamics versus representation

The 2018 process-matrix transformation framework and 2024 reassessment are retained together. Conclusions about whether causal order can evolve depend materially on what the higher-order objects represent. Stage 1 therefore does not infer one intrinsic framework dynamics.

### Composition

Unrestricted tensor products of indefinite-causal objects can fail probability normalization in declared settings. This is a material later-taxonomy burden.

### Simulation versus realization

Restricted/postselected fixed-order simulations coexist with strong deterministic universal simulation obstructions. Simulation equivalence, resource advantage, and physical realization remain separate predicates.
