## 3. Source-search architecture

Search was lane-based, authority-first, and symmetric. Primary papers were preferred for specific results; current specialist reviews were retained where they were needed to map a broad field or current open status. One collaboration result was retained to bound the empirical ceiling. A journal article and its arXiv version count as one scientific source. Search-result snippets, blogs, popular articles, Wikipedia, and AI summaries were not admitted.

The nine mandatory lanes were searched for: foundational structure; duality/nonperturbative structure; holography; compactification/vacua; landscape/selection; swampland; physical realization/cosmology; phenomenology/empirical ceiling; and limitations/open-definition questions. Candidate selection asked, for every major positive result, what domain, construction, conjecture, selection, model, or observational limitation blocks a stronger interpretation.
