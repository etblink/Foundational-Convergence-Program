## 0. Subtraction rule

Before any FCP-6 convergence claim, every AQFT-side structure is assigned to one provenance bucket:

- `AQFT-R` — reformulational ordinary-QFT content;
- `AQFT-G` — generic operator-algebra/category/functor/net/representation/gluing mathematics;
- `AQFT-A` — supplied AQFT axiom or named optional assumption;
- `AQFT-I` — empirical success inherited from concrete QFT/SM models;
- `AQFT-X` — additional source-qualified AQFT residue surviving the FCP-5 reformulation control.

Reduced-NFC resemblance to `AQFT-R`, `AQFT-G`, `AQFT-A`, or `AQFT-I` earns **zero AQFT-specific convergence credit**. Only `AQFT-X` may support a moderate/strong candidate, and only if the independent-derivation, weaker-framework, structure-preservation, physical-bridge and scope tests also pass.

## 1. Excluded AQFT-side material

### AQFT-R — reformulation

Excluded from AQFT-specific convergence credit:

- the bare fact that QFT states may be encoded algebraically;
- expectation-value pairing of states and observables at generic level;
- representation-independent re-expression of ordinary QFT content;
- ordinary relativistic QFT structures already source-bound in the null baseline.

### AQFT-G — generic mathematics

Excluded by the weaker-framework test unless a specific non-generic physical instantiation is separately demonstrated:

- algebras and homomorphisms;
- categories and functors;
- nets/diagrams and inclusions;
- representations and equivalence/isomorphism machinery;
- generic local-to-global/gluing logic;
- generic perturbative/renormalization mathematics;
- generic completely-positive maps or instrument algebra.

### AQFT-A — supplied assumptions

The following are not treated as derived AQFT discoveries merely because they are powerful:

- supplied spacetime region structure;
- isotony/local inclusion conditions;
- Einstein causality/locality where postulated;
- covariance where postulated;
- time-slice structure where additionally assumed;
- model-specific regularity or state-selection hypotheses.

### AQFT-I — empirical inheritance

Concrete QFT/SM masses, couplings, spectra, cross sections, decay rates and other successful numerical predictions remain empirical inheritance unless a distinct abstract-AQFT discriminator is source-bound.

## 2. AQFT-X residue admitted to FCP-6

### X1 — LCQFT cross-background organization

The locally covariant extension organizes a theory as a functor from a category of globally hyperbolic classical spacetimes/admissible embeddings to a category of algebras/morphisms. This is retained only as the physically specialized cross-background model-class architecture after generic functoriality and the supplied background geometry are subtracted.

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-BFV-2003`, `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-FV-2015`.  
**Scope ceiling:** model-class extension; classical Lorentzian geometry remains supplied; not quantum gravity.

### X2 — Relative Cauchy evolution

Under the named LCQFT/time-slice conditions, compact metric/background perturbations define relative Cauchy evolution and a controlled background-response structure. It is not a universal physical-history selector.

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-BFV-2003`, `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-FV-2015`.  
**Scope ceiling:** conditional background-response structure.

### X3 — Natural/global state-selection obstruction

The bounded modern AQFT/LCQFT source set records that a single preferred state cannot generally be selected naturally/covariantly across all spacetimes under the relevant theorem hypotheses. This is a specific selection obstruction, not the generic statement that global choices may fail.

**Source:** `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-FV-2015`.  
**Scope ceiling:** theorem/hypothesis-bounded state-selection obstruction; not absence of admissible state spaces.

### X4 — Localized system–probe measurement architecture

A system QFT and probe QFT may be dynamically coupled in a bounded spacetime region; the scattering construction induces system observables from probe observables and supports causal composition under stated causal-factorization conditions.

**Source:** `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-FV-MEAS-2020`.  
**Scope ceiling:** physically interpreted localized measurement framework; not universal calibration and not independent empirical selection of AQFT.

### X5 — pAQFT interacting/renormalized model construction

The FCP-4/FCP-5 bounded pAQFT source is retained only for the additional interacting-model/renormalized construction architecture identified there, not as a property of minimal Haag–Kastler AQFT and not for generic perturbation/RG mathematics.

**Source:** `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-BFR-2025`.  
**Scope ceiling:** named model-construction extension.

## 3. Interface Sufficiency firewall

No split-property, nuclearity, or other optional AQFT theorem outside the FCP-4 source corpus is imported merely to manufacture an analogue of Reduced-NFC Finite Interface Sufficiency. The bounded Fewster–Verch measurement construction supplies localization and causal factorization, but it does not by itself establish a theorem of the form

`q = Φ ∘ c`

for a finite interface statistic `c` that captures every selected exterior query `q`.

Therefore FCP-6 begins with **no presumed AQFT analogue of Reduced-NFC FIS**.
