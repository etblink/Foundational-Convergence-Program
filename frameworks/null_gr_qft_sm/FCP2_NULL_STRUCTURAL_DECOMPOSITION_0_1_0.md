# 2. K1 — State / configuration carrier

## GR

At FCP-1 scope, GR works with a differentiable spacetime equipped with Lorentzian metric and matter fields. Initial-value formulations further use constraint-compatible initial data on suitable hypersurfaces. The exact mathematical presentation can vary without implying a different physical theory.

**M1:** spacetime differentiable structure and metric-field framework are `PRIMITIVE` relative to the null baseline; particular solutions/initial data are `MODEL_CHOICE` or `EMPIRICALLY_FIXED`.

**M2:** `C2–C3`; no claim that this carrier is the uniquely fundamental ontology of nature.

**M3:** `EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED_TESTED_REGIME + NO_ONTOLOGY_CLAIM`.

## QFT / SM

The SM specifies quantum fields, gauge representations, and quantum-state machinery appropriate to its calculations. Particular Hilbert/Fock/state constructions depend on regime and representation; the FCP baseline does not force one philosophical ontology of fields or particles.

**M1:** SM field content/model is `PRIMITIVE` relative to FCP-1; many numerical parameters/states are `EMPIRICALLY_FIXED`; preparation/initial-state choices are often `MODEL_CHOICE`.

**M2:** `C2–C3`; specific empirically established state content can receive `C5` support without becoming a unique deeper ontology.

**M3:** `PHYSICAL_CONDITIONAL + EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED_TESTED_REGIME`.

## Combined null status

There is no FCP-1 theorem constructing one joint quantum-gravitational state carrier that reduces to both sectors at arbitrary scale.

**Combined status:** `UNDERDETERMINED` for a unified fundamental carrier.

# 3. K2 — Redundancy / physical equivalence

GR and gauge QFT both distinguish representational redundancy from physical difference, but the structures are not declared identical merely because both use quotient-like reasoning.

- GR coordinate descriptions related by admissible diffeomorphic relabeling are not physically distinct merely because coordinates differ.
- SM gauge representatives related by gauge transformations are not physically distinct merely because gauge coordinates differ.
- QFT basis and renormalization-parametrization changes can alter mathematical presentation without altering properly defined physical observables.

This is a **reference decomposition only**. FCP-2 does not claim GR diffeomorphism redundancy and internal gauge redundancy are strongly convergent structures; that would require a future cross-framework equivalence analysis.

**M1:** mostly `SOURCE_DERIVED` once the component theories are declared.

**M2:** `C2–C3`.

**M3:** `FRAMEWORK_INTERNAL + PHYSICAL_CONDITIONAL`; no single deeper equivalence relation established.

# 4. K3 — Allowed transformations

The null baseline contains several transformation classes:

- coordinate/representation changes;
- gauge transformations;
- spacetime/internal symmetries;
- physically allowed interaction channels;
- perturbations/interventions;
- RG/coarse-graining maps.

They must not be collapsed into one undifferentiated category.

Most importantly:

> **The existence of a transformation class does not say which physical transformation occurs.**

That burden belongs to K4.

**M1:** mixture of `SOURCE_DERIVED` and `MODEL_CHOICE`; physical interventions are operationally selected/realized in experiments.

**M2:** generally `C2–C3`.

**M3:** `FRAMEWORK_INTERNAL`; operational completeness is not established.

# 5. K4 — Actual dynamics / history selector

## GR strength

FCP-1 records a genuine distinction from a mere allowed-process framework: given the Einstein-Hilbert action/coupling assumptions and suitable matter/initial-boundary data, Einstein's equations provide actual classical dynamics.

This does not give one unique history without initial/boundary data.

## QFT/SM strength

The SM action and quantum rules determine amplitudes, rates, and evolution conditional on prepared states, parameters, and the measurement/calculation context. This is substantive dynamical structure, not merely a list of legal field transformations.

## Combined limitation

The FCP-1 null competitor is explicitly modular. It does not supply one established UV-complete history selector for quantum matter plus fully dynamical spacetime at arbitrary curvature/energy.

**M1:** sector laws `SOURCE_DERIVED`; initial/boundary/preparation data usually `MODEL_CHOICE`/`EMPIRICALLY_FIXED`; unified law `UNDERDETERMINED`.

**M2:** sector dynamics `C3`; tested predictions `C5`; no unified `C4` claim.

**M3:** `EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED_TESTED_REGIME + UV_STATUS_OPEN`.

# 6. K5 — Observable algebra / measurement interface

The null baseline has mature operational observables rather than merely suggestive formal quantities.

GR examples include clock/redshift comparisons, differential acceleration, orbital/pulsar timing, gravitational-wave strain and waveform quantities.

QFT/SM examples include cross sections, decay widths, branching ratios, masses, asymmetries, event spectra, correlation/rate observables, and oscillation probabilities.

The baseline nevertheless does **not** prove:

> every physically meaningful observable belongs to one unique exhaustive algebra.

This distinction is deliberately preserved for later frameworks.

**M1:** observable definitions mostly `SOURCE_DERIVED`; calibration and parameter estimates `EMPIRICALLY_FIXED`; completeness `UNDERDETERMINED`.

**M2:** `C2–C3`, with specific tested observable relations `C5`.

**M3:** `EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED_TESTED_REGIME + GLOBAL_COMPLETENESS_UNPROVED`.

# 7. K6 — Locality / causal structure

GR supplies a Lorentzian metric/light-cone structure with relativistic causal propagation. Standard relativistic QFT imposes local interaction/microcausal structure over spacetime.

The compatibility of these descriptions in ordinary established regimes is a major practical success, but the null baseline does not derive spacetime locality from a deeper nonspatiotemporal theory.

Entanglement correlation is not treated as a superluminal signalling rule merely because correlations can be nonclassical.

**M1:** spacetime/local field framework `PRIMITIVE` relative to the null baseline; propagation rules `SOURCE_DERIVED`; empirical geometry/propagation constraints `EMPIRICALLY_FIXED`.

**M2:** framework `C3`; tested causal propagation `C5`; deeper origin no `C4` claim.

**M3:** `EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED_TESTED_REGIME + NO_DEEPER_ORIGIN_CLAIM`.

# 8. K7 — Coarse-graining / renormalization / scale relation

QFT provides an especially explicit scale relation through renormalization-group evolution, running couplings, matching, and effective field theory. GR also supports controlled approximations/effective descriptions such as weak-field and post-Newtonian regimes, with broader EFT use where declared.

These structures establish relations among descriptions at different scales. They do not, by themselves, prove that physical ontology changes with scale.

Scheme/truncation dependence must be separated from all-order physical invariance and from empirical running.

**M1:** RG/EFT law structure `SOURCE_DERIVED`; schemes/truncations `MODEL_CHOICE`; physical input scales/couplings `EMPIRICALLY_FIXED`.

**M2:** `C2–C3`; quantitative running can receive `C5`.

**M3:** `EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED_TESTED_REGIME + UV_STATUS_OPEN`.

# 9. K8 — Local-to-global consistency / globalization

The null baseline contains multiple rigorous local-to-global mechanisms, but not one universal globalization doctrine.
