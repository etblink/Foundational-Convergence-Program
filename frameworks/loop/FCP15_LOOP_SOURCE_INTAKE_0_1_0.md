## 0. Controlling question

> **What is the smallest source-qualified loop/spin-network/spinfoam framework object that can be compared under frozen FCP K1–K10 without pooling distinct formulations, importing generic mathematics as physical content, or treating optional dynamics/reconstruction machinery as universal core?**

FCP-15 answers this as a source-intake/taxonomy task only. It performs no loop/null, loop/NFC, loop/CST, loop/AQFT, loop/CQM or loop/GPTOPT E1–E5 comparison.

## 1. Framework disposition

`FW-LOOP` remains one top-level FCP family, but it requires persistent internal subframework labels:

- `LOOP-CANON` — canonical holonomy-flux / spin-network / constraint formulation;
- `LOOP-COVAR` — covariant/spinfoam amplitude formulation, represented at highest source specificity by EPRL-type models and their refinements.

Taxonomy result:

`FCP15_LOOP_TAXONOMY = OUTCOME_B_INTERNAL_SUBFRAMEWORK_DISTINCTION`

`FRAMEWORK_SPLIT_CANDIDATE = 0`

Reason: the source corpus establishes shared quantum-geometry/boundary-state lineage and a nontrivial EPRL bridge to canonical LQG on a fixed graph, while canonical and covariant dynamics are constructed differently and no source in the bounded corpus establishes whole-sector identity, unique common dynamics, or complete continuum equivalence. These differences require persistent internal labels but do not yet satisfy the FCP-4 burden for separate top-level framework IDs.

## 2. Layer decomposition

FCP-15 freezes the following layers. Results from a higher layer are never back-projected into a lower one.

### `L0` — generic mathematical infrastructure

Includes, according to the selected formulation:

- graphs and combinatorial complexes;
- Hilbert-space and representation-theoretic machinery;
- `SU(2)` and Lorentz-group representation theory;
- generic constrained-Hamiltonian methods;
- path-integral / BF / simplicial regularization machinery;
- generic category/topology/combinatorics.

These structures earn zero loop-specific physical credit by themselves.

### `L1` — loop quantum geometry / kinematics

Source-qualified canonical structure includes:

- connection/holonomy and triad/flux variables;
- cylindrical/graph-dependent states;
- spin-network bases;
- kinematical Hilbert-space structure;
- internal gauge and spatial-diffeomorphism treatment at their appropriate stages;
- geometric area/volume operators with discrete spectra at kinematical scope.

The `L1` physical interpretation is quantum spatial geometry, but `L1` is not the physical Hilbert space and does not supply complete dynamics.

### `L2` — constraints / physical-state selection

Source-qualified constraints include Gauss, spatial-diffeomorphism and scalar/Hamiltonian constraints in canonical formulations. Quantum Hamiltonian constructions exist, but defining/interpreting the complete solution space, physical inner product and observables remains a nontrivial dynamics/physical-state problem.

> **GAUGE/DIFFEOMORPHISM REDUCTION != DYNAMICAL SELECTION.**

### `L3` — dynamics

Two source-bound routes are retained separately:

- `LOOP-CANON`: Hamiltonian/scalar-constraint quantization, including QSD and later canonical dynamics programs;
- `LOOP-COVAR`: transition amplitudes/spinfoam sums, including EPRL-type amplitudes on declared two-complexes/triangulations.

No bounded source establishes one unique framework-wide physical history law across `FW-LOOP`.

> **KINEMATICAL SPIN-NETWORK STRUCTURE != COMPLETE PHYSICAL DYNAMICS.**

> **AN ALLOWED SPINFOAM OR CONSTRAINT OPERATOR != A UNIQUE PHYSICAL HISTORY LAW.**

### `L4` — semiclassical / continuum / GR recovery

Source-qualified partial bridges include:

- large-spin/simplex asymptotics with Lorentzian Regge-action phases under declared boundary-data assumptions;
- lowest-order/large-spin metric-correlation calculations compared with perturbative graviton behavior;
- coarse-graining / refinement / renormalization programs;
- a 2026 model-independent structural analysis of continuum spin-foam limits, including a strong-convergence obstruction and a weaker distributional/rigging-map construction under assumptions.

These do not establish complete, unique recovery of continuum 3+1 GR with all physical observables.

> **DISCRETE GEOMETRIC SPECTRUM != DERIVATION OF CLASSICAL SPACETIME.**

> **FIXED-COMPLEX REGGE ASYMPTOTICS != COMPLETE CONTINUUM GR RECOVERY.**

### `L5` — matter, cosmology and optional physical extensions

Not base `FW-LOOP` for FCP-15:

- loop quantum cosmology;
- isolated-horizon/black-hole model packages;
- particular matter couplings;
- master-constraint variants;
- selected coherent-state/semi-classical packages;
- group-field-theory reformulations/extensions;
- named spin-foam variants beyond what is needed to bind `LOOP-COVAR`;
- phenomenological modified-dispersion/Lorentz-violation models.

### `L6` — phenomenology / empirical status

The bounded phenomenology review documents multiple proposed Planck-scale/loop-inspired observational routes, while emphasizing that the derivational link from fundamental LQG to specific phenomenological models is incomplete. FCP-15 adds no direct empirical/observational source record.

Framework status:

`NO_FRAMEWORK_LEVEL_EMPIRICAL_DISCRIMINATOR_IDENTIFIED`.

## 5. Barbero–Immirzi parameter control

The modern source record treats the Barbero–Immirzi parameter `γ` as a one-parameter quantization ambiguity in the canonical construction; geometric spectra and EPRL constructions can depend on it. FCP-15 therefore records:

`BARBERO_IMMIRZI_PARAMETER = FRAMEWORK/MODEL_INPUT_WITH_PHYSICAL_CALIBRATION_BURDEN`

No FCP-15 claim treats fitting or selecting `γ` in an extension as an independent base-framework prediction.
