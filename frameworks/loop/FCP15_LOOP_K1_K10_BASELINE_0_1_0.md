## 2. K1 — kinematical carrier versus physical states

### Canonical

The bounded canonical sources support a background-independent connection formulation with holonomies/fluxes and a kinematical Hilbert space organized by graph-dependent cylindrical functions and spin-network states.

Spin-network labels and graph structure are source-qualified quantum-geometric state data at `L1`.

They are **not** automatically:

- solutions of the Hamiltonian constraint;
- elements of a completely constructed physical Hilbert space;
- one unique continuum spacetime state;
- detector-level physical configurations.

### Covariant

In source-bound spinfoam models, spin-network states occur as boundary states while labelled two-complexes/spinfoams encode histories/amplitude data.

The EPRL finite-`γ` source establishes a strong fixed-graph boundary-space relation to canonical LQG. FCP-15 does not extrapolate this to all covariant models or a complete canonical physical-Hilbert equivalence.

> **KINEMATICAL SPIN NETWORK != COMPLETE PHYSICAL STATE.**

## 3. K2 — reduction is not dynamics

Canonical LQG contains internal gauge and spatial-diffeomorphism structure and a scalar/Hamiltonian constraint associated with the remaining generally covariant dynamics burden.

FCP-15 distinguishes:

- gauge invariance;
- spatial diffeomorphism invariance/equivalence;
- Hamiltonian/scalar-constraint solution;
- physical inner product;
- graph/refinement representation choices.

No source allows these to be pooled into one generic “redundancy solved” status.

> **GAUGE/DIFFEOMORPHISM REDUCTION != DYNAMICAL SELECTION.**

## 4. K3 — allowed actions and amplitudes

Source-qualified process/change structures include:

- constraint actions on canonical spin-network states, including graph-changing actions in QSD-type constructions;
- gauge/diffeomorphism actions;
- spinfoam amplitudes/transitions between boundary spin networks in selected covariant models;
- refinement/coarse-graining maps at `L4` program scope.

These structures are scientifically substantive but formulation-dependent.

> **ALLOWED TRANSFORMATION != ACTUAL HISTORY.**

## 5. K4 — dynamics is the principal unresolved framework-wide selector

### `LOOP-CANON`

QSD and later canonical programs provide concrete quantum-constraint constructions. The modern source record nevertheless keeps material burdens open:

- regularization/quantization choices;
- interpretation of solutions;
- physical inner product;
- complete observable algebra;
- faithful/physically adequate implementation of the constraint structure;
- one uniquely selected physical Hamiltonian/history law.

The 2021 modern review explicitly treats full LQG quantum dynamics as incomplete.

### `LOOP-COVAR`

Spinfoam models provide concrete covariant amplitude rules. EPRL-type models are central but are not synonymous with all possible spinfoam dynamics.

Open burdens include:

- model/constraint implementation choices;
- dependence on the selected two-complex/discretization;
- sum/refinement/continuum construction;
- physical-Hilbert interpretation;
- whole-sector relation to canonical dynamics.

FCP-15 therefore records:

`FRAMEWORK_WIDE_UNIQUE_DYNAMICS = OPEN`.

## 6. K5 — geometric spectra and physical observables

The area-operator source proves a discrete spectrum on the **kinematical** Hilbert space. This is a strong quantum-geometry result.

FCP-15 does not infer that the spectrum is automatically:

- a Dirac observable;
- an experimentally measured minimum area;
- invariant under all dynamics/formulation choices;
- a complete measurement theory.

Physical observables in a generally covariant theory require the physical-state/dynamics/relational bridge appropriate to the chosen formulation.

> **OPERATOR SPECTRUM != DIRECTLY MEASURED OBSERVABLE WITHOUT A PHYSICAL BRIDGE.**

## 7. K6 — combinatorial adjacency is not spacetime locality

Graphs and two-complexes organize quantum states/amplitudes. Their adjacency relations are mathematical/combinatorial data.

FCP-15 identifies no framework-wide theorem equating raw adjacency with:

- physical spatial distance;
- spacelike separation;
- Lorentzian causal order;
- microcausality;
- no-signalling operational structure.

Background independence is not itself a physical locality law.

> **GRAPH ADJACENCY != PHYSICAL SPACETIME LOCALITY.**

## 8. K7 — refinement, coarse graining and continuum scale

The spin-foam literature contains an explicit background-independent coarse-graining/RG program relating fine and coarse amplitudes through boundary data. This is genuine `K7` structure and is stronger than merely observing multiple graph scales.

However, the 2020 review reports that full 4D EPRL/FK coarse graining and the continuum/universality problem remain open at its scope.

The 2026 continuum paper adds a source-qualified structural advance:

- continuum limits can be formalized model-independently through ordered triangulations/combinatorial data;
- sufficiently strong convergence assumptions can force a topological theory;
- a weaker distributional notion can construct a rigging map and physical Hilbert structure under the paper's axioms.

This is not a theorem that the physical 4D EPRL model has reached continuum GR.

> **REFINEMENT != RENORMALIZATION WITHOUT A SOURCE-BOUND SCALE MAP AND PRESERVED PHYSICS.**

## 9. K8 — discrete composition versus physical globalization

Spinfoam gluing and boundary-amplitude composition provide nontrivial local-to-global mathematical structure. Canonical graph/spin-network constructions also support composition and refinement of state descriptions.

But FCP-15 preserves the distinction among:

1. gluing finite building blocks;
2. consistent amplitudes across regulators;
3. a continuum physical Hilbert/amplitude;
4. emergence/recovery of 3+1 Einstein dynamics and observables.

Evidence for step 1 does not automatically establish 2–4.

> **SPINFOAM GLUING != COMPLETE PHYSICAL GLOBALIZATION.**

## 10. K9 — physical realization and the Immirzi burden

Loop quantum geometry provides explicit formulas in which spin labels, Planck units and the Barbero–Immirzi parameter enter geometric spectra. Semiclassical/coherent-state and spinfoam asymptotic calculations build partial bridges toward classical geometry.

FCP-15 records `γ` as a quantization/model input with a physical calibration burden. The 2021 review describes it as a one-parameter quantization ambiguity whose physical value requires external fixing/observation rather than derivation from the kinematical representation alone.

Partial `L4` bridges include:

- Regge-action asymptotics for suitable Lorentzian simplex data;

- structural continuum-limit constructions.

Absent framework-wide from the bounded corpus:
