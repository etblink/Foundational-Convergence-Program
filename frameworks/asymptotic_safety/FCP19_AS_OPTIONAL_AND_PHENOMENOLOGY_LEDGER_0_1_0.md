# FCP-19 — Asymptotic Safety Optional Structure and Phenomenology Ledger

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Framework:** `FW-AS`  
**Status:** OPTIONALITY / EMPIRICAL-BOUNDARY CANDIDATE

## 0. Purpose

This ledger prevents phenomenologically interesting, trajectory-specific, truncation-specific or extension-specific structures from being silently promoted to unavoidable base-`FW-AS` predictions.

Required distinction:

- `DIRECT_FRAMEWORK_DISCRIMINATOR`
- `MODEL_OR_TRAJECTORY_SPECIFIC`
- `OPTIONAL_EXTENSION_PHENOMENOLOGY`
- `PARAMETER_CONSTRAINT`
- `EMPIRICALLY_INHERITED`
- `NO_CURRENT_DISCRIMINATOR`

## 1. Base framework versus optional realization

Base `FW-AS` commits to the UV fixed-point / finite-critical-surface hypothesis at the source-qualified level. It does not uniquely fix, merely from that hypothesis:

- one finite truncation;
- one regulator;
- one field parametrization;
- one RG trajectory;
- one cosmological history;
- one black-hole improvement prescription;
- one matter extension;
- one numerical low-energy parameter set;
- one detector-level observable prediction.

Therefore:

> **THEORETICAL PREDICTIVITY != A UNIQUE EMPIRICAL PREDICTION.**

## 2. Gravity-matter compatibility

Sources: `SRC-FCP19-AS-DEP-2014`, `SRC-FCP19-AS-MPR-2016`, `SRC-FCP19-AS-EICHHORN-2026`.

### Source-bound content

- selected truncations support interacting gravitational fixed points with matter;
- the Standard Model is compatible in studied settings;
- some analyses produce matter-content bounds;
- other dynamical implementations find much weaker or absent bounds within their validity range;
- gravitational fluctuations can alter matter beta functions and fixed-point structure.

### Classification

`MODEL_OR_TRAJECTORY_SPECIFIC` + `PARAMETER_CONSTRAINT`.

### Not carried as base-framework prediction

- exact exclusion of all GUTs;
- exact prediction of Standard Model field content;
- derivation of masses/couplings solely from `AS-H`;
- experimentally established particle prediction unique to AS.

Permanent control:

> **MATTER COMPATIBILITY != STANDARD MODEL DERIVATION.**

## 3. Scale and hierarchy phenomenology

Source: `SRC-FCP19-AS-GRS-2019`.

### Source-bound content

Selected RG trajectories can generate characteristic transition/freeze-out scales and connect trans-Planckian running to classical regimes. The review discusses possible cosmological determination of remaining parameters.

### Classification

`MODEL_OR_TRAJECTORY_SPECIFIC` and partly `PARAMETER_CONSTRAINT`.

### Calibration firewall

The observed values of Newton's constant, the cosmological constant or combinations such as `GΛ` may be used to anchor a trajectory. Such use is calibration and is not an independent prediction of the base framework.

Permanent control:

> **OBSERVED_PARAMETER_USED_TO_SELECT_A_TRAJECTORY != ASYMPTOTIC_SAFETY PREDICTION.**

## 4. Black-hole phenomenology

Source: `SRC-FCP19-AS-PLATANIA-2024`.

### Source-bound content

The bounded review covers:

- RG-improved static and rotating black-hole models;
- possible short-distance modifications;
- singularity-resolution proposals;
- Buchdahl/compactness effects;
- improved prescriptions designed to reduce coordinate/scale-setting ambiguity;
- first-principles and path-integral-motivated constraints.

### Classification

Predominantly `OPTIONAL_EXTENSION_PHENOMENOLOGY` or `MODEL_OR_TRAJECTORY_SPECIFIC`.

### Main limitation

A large fraction of the black-hole literature requires an RG-improvement prescription that maps RG scale to spacetime data. Different prescriptions can change the resulting geometry, and the review explicitly treats closing the gap to first-principles RG computations as ongoing work.

Permanent control:

> **RG-IMPROVED BLACK HOLE != UNAVOIDABLE BASE-FRAMEWORK SOLUTION.**

## 5. Cosmology and inflation

Sources: `SRC-FCP19-AS-FKLR-2018`, `SRC-FCP19-AS-EICHHORN-2026`, `SRC-FCP19-AS-GRS-2019`.

### Source-bound content

Some fixed-point effective actions/truncations admit inflationary or cosmologically interesting solutions, and modern reviews discuss possible early-universe and late-time signatures.

### Classification

`MODEL_OR_TRAJECTORY_SPECIFIC` / `OPTIONAL_EXTENSION_PHENOMENOLOGY`.

### Not carried

- a unique AS inflation model;
- a unique scalar spectral index prediction forced by the base framework;
- unique late-time dark-energy prediction;
- a base-framework falsification rule independent of truncation/trajectory choices.

## 10. Empirical-inheritance firewall for later phases

FCP-19 performs no AS/null comparison, but it freezes a necessary future control:

> **IF AN AS TRAJECTORY RECOVERS TESTED GR/QFT BEHAVIOR, THE EMPIRICAL SUCCESS OF THAT RECOVERED THEORY IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY INDEPENDENT AS EVIDENCE.**

The actual relation must be classified in a separately authorized pairwise phase.

## 11. Optionality summary

The source corpus supports substantial physical and phenomenological development beyond abstract fixed-point searches. Yet the route from the UV hypothesis to a concrete observable normally passes through one or more of:

- truncation choice;
- RG trajectory choice;
- matching/calibration;
- signature/realization assumptions;
- matter-model assumptions;
- scale-identification/improvement prescription;
- cosmological/black-hole model choices.

These are not treated as defects by default. They are recorded because they determine whether a proposed observable consequence binds the base framework.
