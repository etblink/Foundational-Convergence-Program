## 0. Purpose

This ledger records fixed-point, relevant-direction, robustness, matter, signature and continuum evidence without pooling distinct truncations into a fictional exact fixed point.

> **FIXED POINT IN A TRUNCATION != FIXED POINT OF THE COMPLETE THEORY.**

## 1. Framework-level hypothesis

`AS-H` requires an appropriate interacting UV fixed point together with a finite-dimensional UV critical surface for the physically relevant theory space.

The logical predictivity statement is conditional:

`FINITE_DIMENSIONAL_UV_CRITICAL_SURFACE -> FINITE_NUMBER_OF_FREE_RELEVANT_PARAMETERS`.

The corpus source-binds this implication. It does not source-bind the exact dimension of the physical complete gravity/matter critical surface.

## 4. Critical-surface / predictivity status

### 4.1 Logical framework statement

At an interacting fixed point, linearized RG flow defines critical exponents and relevant/irrelevant directions. If the UV critical surface is finite-dimensional, specifying finitely many relevant coordinates fixes a trajectory inside it.

Status: `SOURCE_BOUND_FORMAL`.

### 4.2 Truncation evidence

Several retained truncations report a small number of relevant directions, commonly around two or three in the specific bases studied. Higher-order `f(R)` and Ricci-tensor studies support a near-Gaussian ordering for sufficiently high canonical dimension.

Status: `AS-L3_EVIDENCE`.

### 4.3 Exact physical dimension

Field-parametrization dependence can change the apparent relevant-direction count. Matter and background/dynamical treatments can shift fixed-point structures. The complete gravity/matter theory space has not been controlled exhaustively.

Status: `OPEN`.

Therefore:

`FINITE_RELEVANT_DIRECTIONS_IN_TRUNCATIONS != COMPLETE_PREDICTIVITY_THEOREM`.

## 6. Continuum-limit status

The bounded corpus contains:

- formal continuum criteria;
- many fixed-point approximations;
- selected global/UV–IR trajectories;
- increasing dynamical-fluctuation and Lorentzian work.

It does not provide one source-qualified proof that the complete realistic gravity-plus-matter theory possesses the required global trajectory with all physical positivity/unitarity/observable conditions.

Status: `CONDITIONAL_WITH_STRONG_FIXED_POINT_EVIDENCE`.

Permanent control:

> **NON-GAUSSIAN FIXED POINT != COMPLETE CONTINUUM THEORY WITHOUT THE REQUIRED LIMIT/TRAJECTORY CONTROL.**
