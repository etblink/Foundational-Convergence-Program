## 2. K1 carrier

The minimal configuration is the causal set itself, not its labels, not an embedding, and not a growth history. Dynamics-specific formulations may instead use spaces of finite stems or completed growth histories, but those belong to `C2/C3`.

**Primary core-population count:** K1 is core-populated.

## 3. K2 redundancy

Relabellings preserving the order do not create new unlabeled causal sets. In CSG the demand for discrete general covariance makes path/label bookkeeping subordinate to covariant content. This is not the same claim as saying two different causal sets with embeddings into one continuum are physically identical.

**Primary core-population count:** K2 is core-populated.

## 4. K3 allowed processes

An order-preserving map is not automatically time evolution. One-element growth is a candidate dynamical transition only after a growth law is supplied. Random sprinkling is an approximation/sampling prescription, not actual fundamental growth.

**Extension-dependent:** K3's physical process content is materially extension-dependent.

## 5. K4 actual dynamics

Minimal CST is kinematic. Rideout–Sorkin obtain a broad stochastic CSG family under Markovianity, discrete general covariance and Bell-causality-type conditions; the family retains free couplings and does not uniquely select realistic continuum spacetime.

Quantum dynamics remains incomplete. Surya–Zalel provide nontrivial covariance/measure-extension results for complex growth models. Current 2026 work on quantum Bell causality explicitly remains a first-step constraint on candidate noncommutative QSG algebras rather than a solved dynamics.

Framework-wide status:

`NO_UNIQUE_SOURCE_SELECTED_QUANTUM_DYNAMICS`.

**Extension-dependent:** K4.

## 6. K5 observables

CST has many covariant combinatorial quantities. In CSG, stem sets acquire a genuine covariant event/observable role. Separately, chain length, interval counts, dimension estimators, d'Alembertians and curvature/action functionals can carry continuum interpretation under assumptions.

## 7. K6 causal structure and Lorentz compatibility

K6 is the most distinctive core key. Causal order is primitive/interpreted, while the continuum Lorentzian causal structure is a realization target rather than something obtained from generic poset mathematics alone.

Poisson sprinklings provide Lorentz-invariant probability law/continuum approximations. The BHS theorem blocks an intrinsic covariant preferred-direction selector from a Minkowski sprinkling, but it does not make each random realization pointwise invariant under the Lorentz group.

Characteristic nonlocality follows from combining discreteness with Lorentz compatibility: finite-valency nearest-neighbor graph intuition cannot simply be imported.

**Primary core-population count:** K6 is core-populated.

## 8. K7 scale

CST has a discreteness density and scale-sensitive reconstruction, but FCP-9 finds no minimal framework-wide RG flow, fixed point or universality theorem. Planckian density is a physical/model assumption in the bounded framework rather than a source-derived numerical prediction.

**Extension-dependent:** K7.

## 9. K8 continuum/globalization

The continuum-reconstruction problem is intrinsic to CST's physical identity. Order/number estimators successfully reconstruct multiple continuum invariants for manifoldlike causal sets, reaching R1/R2 and source-specific pieces of R3.

But three propositions remain distinct:

1. `C` has a faithful continuum embedding;
2. that continuum is unique up to acceptable discreteness-scale differences;
3. CST dynamics generates such manifoldlike `C` with high probability.

Only (1) is routinely realized by the standard sprinkling construction. Traditional finite uniqueness remains refined/open at the framework level. Müller's 2025 preprint proves specific mathematical formulations, including a countable injectivity result, but expressly cautions against identifying those results with the full historical CST target.

Kleitman–Rothschild asymptotics supply a negative witness: generic large finite posets are highly non-manifoldlike, so the physical sector is not selected by kinematics alone.

- `TRADITIONAL_FINITE_CONTINUUM_UNIQUENESS_OPEN_REFINED`;
- `MANIFOLDLIKENESS_SELECTION_OPEN`;
- `DIMENSION_SELECTION_OPEN`;

## 10. K9 physical realization

CST has more than a metaphorical bridge to continuum geometry: causal/volume reconstruction theorems and manifoldlike estimators/operators are substantive. Benincasa–Dowker-type operators give controlled continuum behavior for slowly varying fields/curvature in declared settings.

But no bounded source establishes full `R5` recovery of realistic 3+1-dimensional GR plus Standard Model matter and calibrated observables from one selected CST quantum dynamics.

Matter fields remain additions to the minimal causal set. The discreteness density is not derived uniquely.

**Primary core-population count:** K9 is core-populated at the level of the defining order/number continuum bridge, while advanced realization is extension-dependent.
