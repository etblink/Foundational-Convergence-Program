## 1. Minimal core

The BLMS/modern CST baseline replaces continuum spacetime at the fundamental level by a locally finite partial order `(C, ≺)`.

Using Surya's convention, a causal set satisfies:

1. acyclicity/partial-order consistency;
2. transitivity;
3. local finiteness: every order interval between two elements contains finitely many elements.

Physical interpretation is source-qualified but not mathematically automatic:

- order is interpreted as proto-causal structure;
- local finiteness encodes fundamental discreteness;
- in a faithful continuum approximation, causal order tracks continuum causality and element count tracks spacetime volume.

Permanent control:

> **CAUSAL ORDER + LOCAL FINITENESS != CONTINUUM SPACETIME.**

## 4. Order + number control

Continuum Lorentzian results support the idea that causal structure determines conformal/topological information under appropriate continuum hypotheses, while volume information fixes the remaining scale information in the motivating CST picture.

FCP-9 therefore permits the slogan `order + number` only as a **continuum-reconstruction program**, not as a theorem that every finite causal set uniquely determines a Lorentzian spacetime.

## 5. Hauptvermutung status

The 2019 review reports no full proof of the traditional CST Hauptvermutung and substantial observable-by-observable evidence. A 2025 preprint (current v2 Dec 2025) sharpens the question into precise mathematical formulations, finding one formulation false, another true, and proving a countable injectivity result. The author explicitly cautions that these results likely do not yet coincide with the full historical CST reconstruction target.

FCP-9 status:

`TRADITIONAL_FINITE_CONTINUUM_UNIQUENESS = OPEN/REFINED`.

Specific sharpened/countable results are `SOURCE_DERIVED`; they do not justify a blanket `HAUPTVERMUTUNG_SOLVED` label.

## 6. Manifoldlikeness

The space of locally finite/finite posets is vastly larger than the manifoldlike sector. Generic finite-poset asymptotics (Kleitman–Rothschild) provide a reusable counterpressure against any inference that causal-set kinematics alone selects low-dimensional spacetime.

Thus:

> **KINEMATIC INCLUSION OF MANIFOLDLIKE CAUSAL SETS != DYNAMICAL EXPLANATION OF MANIFOLDLIKENESS.**

## 7. Lorentz-compatible discreteness

Poisson sprinkling is a continuum-approximation device/ensemble, not the definition of CST and not fundamental dynamics. The Bombelli–Henson–Sorkin theorem shows, for the declared sprinkling setting, that no intrinsic equivariant construction can select a preferred spacetime direction.

FCP-9 records:

- ensemble/law is Lorentz invariant;
- a random realization is not literally invariant under every Lorentz transformation;
- no covariantly extractable preferred frame follows from the sprinkling alone;
- finite-valency local-neighbor structure is incompatible with exact Lorentz invariance in the same setting, producing characteristic nonlocality.

## 9. Physical realization

Source-qualified bridges include, under explicit manifoldlike/continuum assumptions:

- order ↔ continuum causal order;
- cardinality ↔ spacetime volume;
- chain/order invariants ↔ geometric estimators;
- causal-set d'Alembertian ↔ continuum wave operator;
- Benincasa–Dowker curvature/action ↔ continuum curvature/action in appropriate limits.

These are meaningful results, but they do not by themselves supply:

- unique continuum reconstruction;
- 3+1-dimension selection;
- topology selection;
- Standard Model matter;
- complete calibration;
- fundamental discreteness-scale prediction.
