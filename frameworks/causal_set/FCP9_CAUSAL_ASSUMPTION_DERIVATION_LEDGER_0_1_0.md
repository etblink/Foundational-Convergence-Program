## 2. Continuum-smuggling controls

A result obtained after assuming a faithful embedding/manifoldlike causal set receives credit for **reconstruction conditional on the bridge**, not for deriving manifoldlikeness itself.

Permanent distinctions:

> **FAITHFUL EMBEDDING != UNIQUE CONTINUUM.**

> **MANIFOLDLIKE INPUT != DYNAMICAL MANIFOLD SELECTION.**

> **CONTINUUM OPERATOR LIMIT != UNIQUE QUANTUM DYNAMICS.**

## 3. Dynamics controls

CSG is a source-derived stochastic **family** under named assumptions. Its external growth label/construction order is not automatically an observable physical time variable; discrete general covariance removes label dependence from physical predictions in the intended formulation.

QSG/quantum-measure work is retained as a serious dynamics program, but FCP-9 does not promote partial covariance/extension results to a complete theory.

## 4. Phenomenology controls

Every C5/C6 item carries an exact model boundary. Negative observational constraints remain preserved as genuine results even when they do not constrain the whole framework.

Permanent rule:

> **MODEL-SPECIFIC PHENOMENOLOGY != FRAMEWORK-LEVEL PREDICTION.**

## 5. Ontology control

Terms such as `event`, `spacetime atom`, `birth`, `growth`, and `becoming` do not by themselves establish empirical ontology. Mathematical construction, physical interpretation, and empirical selection remain separate columns in FCP.
