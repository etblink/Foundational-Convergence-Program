## 1. Taxonomy verdict

The current label `FW-CAUSAL = causal-set / order-theoretic approaches` is **too broad if read literally**. The source-bound causal set theory (CST) program is a specific proposal descended from Bombelli–Lee–Meyer–Sorkin (BLMS): fundamental configurations are locally finite partial orders whose order is interpreted as proto-causal and whose cardinality supplies the discreteness/volume side of the continuum approximation.

Surya's modern synthesis explicitly distinguishes this program from other poset/order approaches whose continuum requirement, covariance doctrine, additional degrees of freedom, or dynamical commitments differ.

Accordingly:

`FRAMEWORK_SPLIT_CANDIDATE = 1`.

FCP-9 does **not** silently change the register ID. It source-binds **causal set theory proper as the controlled CST subset of `FW-CAUSAL`** and recommends a later bounded taxonomy operation before `FW-CAUSAL` is used as a cross-framework comparator.

Suggested future split resolution, not enacted here:

- `FW-CST` — BLMS causal set theory proper;
- a separately source-qualified causal/order family only if later intake establishes a coherent adjacent competitor.

## 2. Classical sequential growth

**Verdict:** extension/dynamics layer of CST, not a separate FCP framework at current scope.

Reasons:

- carrier/sample space remains causal sets;
- physical domain remains discrete causal spacetime;
- the new commitment is a stochastic transition law over causal-set growth histories;
- Rideout–Sorkin derive a **family** of Markovian classical sequential growth (CSG) models from covariance/causality conditions, rather than replacing CST kinematics with a different primitive theory.

Therefore CSG belongs to source layer `C2`, not the minimal `C0` core.

## 3. Quantum measure / quantum sequential growth

**Verdict:** provisional quantum-dynamics program within CST, not a separate FCP framework at current scope.

Quantum measure/decoherence-functional and quantum sequential growth (QSG) work changes the measure/dynamical law on histories but retains causal sets as the intended spacetime configurations. Current sources establish nontrivial covariance/extension results and increasingly constrained candidate transition algebras, but not one complete, empirically selected quantum dynamics that produces realistic 3+1-dimensional manifoldlike GR plus matter.

Therefore QSG belongs to `C3`; it must not be projected back into the kinematic `C0` definition.

## 4. Broader causal/order approaches

Generic causal orders, locally finite posets, energetic causal sets, or other order-theoretic spacetime proposals do not automatically belong to CST. They can differ materially in:

- whether local finiteness is fundamental;
- whether continuum Lorentzian approximation is a defining burden;
- whether cardinality represents volume;
- whether extra labels/energies/momenta are primitive;
- dynamics/history selection;
- empirical targets.

FCP-9 does not source-bind those adjacent approaches and does not treat their strongest results as CST results.

## 5. Minimal CST identity frozen for FCP-9

The controlled causal-set object is a pair `(C, ≺)` satisfying the source convention's poset/acyclicity conditions and local finiteness. The physical interpretation is an additional source-qualified commitment:

- order `≺` ↔ proto-causal relation;
- local finiteness/cardinality ↔ fundamental spacetime discreteness and continuum volume in a faithful approximation.

This does **not** imply:

- arbitrary causal sets are manifoldlike;
- one causal set uniquely fixes one continuum spacetime at all scales;
- a growth order is observable physical time;
- Poisson sprinkling is fundamental dynamics;
- CST already supplies quantum dynamics.
