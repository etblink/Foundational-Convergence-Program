## 0. Scope and verdict

FCP-7 decomposes the already source-bound generalized-probabilistic / operational-probabilistic family before any NFC–GPTOPT comparison.

Controlling result:

> **GPTOPT IS A GENERAL OPERATIONAL META-FRAMEWORK THAT CONTAINS QUANTUM THEORY AS A SPECIAL CASE BUT DOES NOT SELECT IT WITHOUT ADDITIONAL STRUCTURAL/RECONSTRUCTION PRINCIPLES OR EMPIRICAL RESTRICTION.**

The framework remains coherent as one bounded FCP family at this source scope. GPT and OPT presentations differ in mathematical emphasis, but the bounded corpus does not establish materially distinct physical domains, empirical burdens, or allowed model classes sufficient to require separate framework IDs.

`FRAMEWORK_SPLIT_CANDIDATE = 0`.

## 1. Frozen G0–G6 layer decomposition

### G0 — Operational skeleton

Systems, preparations/states, transformations/events, effects/measurements, experimental outcomes and probability assignments. OPT presentations foreground sequential/parallel process composition; GPT presentations often encode the same operational content through state/effect geometry.

### G1 — Convex/probabilistic structure

In standard finite-dimensional GPT presentations, randomization/mixing motivates convex state sets; effects are affine probability-valued functionals and normalized states/effects admit ordered-vector-space/cone descriptions.

Convex analysis itself is generic mathematics. Physical content enters through which state/effect/transformation sets are declared admissible.

### G2 — Composite-system structure

Composite systems, nonsignalling constraints, tensor-product bounds and local tomography live here. The key FCP-7 result is that **local component state spaces do not uniquely select the global composite theory**. Even under tomographic locality, the bounded source corpus permits inequivalent composites between minimal and maximal tensor products.

### G3 — Reconstruction axioms

Additional principles used to narrow GPTOPT toward quantum or another subfamily. In the bounded corpus these include, source-dependently, Hardy's continuity/reversible-transformation axiom, purification, and the reconstruction package summarized by Müller (Tomographic Locality, Continuous Reversibility, Subspace Axiom).

No principle from one reconstruction lineage is silently pooled into another.

### G4 — Quantum embedding

Finite-dimensional quantum theory is an admissible GPT/OPT model: density operators, effects, completely positive transformations and Born-rule probabilities arise in the quantum instantiation. This embedding does not make those structures primitive for the whole family.

### G5 — Generalized/post-quantum theory space

The source-bound theory space includes nonquantum models such as boxworld/PR-box-type theories and other convex operational models. Their mathematical admissibility is not evidence of physical realization.

### G6 — Empirical/physical restriction

FCP-7 adds one bounded empirical source, `SRC-FCP7-GPT-MAZUREK-2021`, because FCP-4 contained no direct experimental GPT-theory-space record. GPT tomography of single-photon polarization constrains deviations from the quantum geometry in that experimental domain without presuming quantum state/measurement tomography.

This is **empirical narrowing of a GPT possibility space**, not confirmation of the GPTOPT meta-framework and not global empirical selection of quantum theory.

## 2. GPT versus OPT subtraditions

### GPT presentation

Typically foregrounds convex normalized state spaces, ordered vector spaces/cones, affine effects and geometry of allowed transformations.

### OPT presentation

Typically foregrounds systems, tests/events/processes, sequential and parallel composition, and probability assignments to circuits.

### FCP-7 split test

The bounded sources support substantial translation/overlap at the FCP K1–K10 level. Differences matter for presentation and theorem hypotheses, but FCP-7 does not find a material divergence in physical scope or empirical burden that would justify two framework IDs.

Result:

`NO_FRAMEWORK_SPLIT_CANDIDATE_AT_CURRENT_SCOPE`.

A future split remains possible if a source-bound analysis demonstrates materially different allowed model classes or K1–K10 commitments.

## 3. Minimal core and non-core firewall

### Minimal bounded core

- operational systems/configurations;
- state/preparation equivalence through outcome statistics, where the formulation is operationally separated;
- effects/measurements and probability rules;
- allowed transformations/processes;
- convex mixing in standard GPT presentations;
- some declared composition rule when composite systems are considered.

### Not generic base consequences

The following must remain explicit assumptions, subtheory choices or reconstruction principles:

- purification;
- local tomography;
- continuous reversibility;
- Hardy's quantum-selecting continuity/reversibility axiom;
- particular tensor/composite products;
- no-restriction hypotheses;
- spacetime/Lorentzian causality;
- one universal dynamics/history selector;
- a unique quantum state space;
- a unique physical effect set;
- complete physical calibration.

## 4. K1–K10 summary

### K1 — State/configuration carrier

**Base:** operational states/preparations represented, in standard GPT form, by points of a convex normalized state space or cone. Operational equivalence can identify preparation procedures with identical probabilities against the chosen effect set.

**Quantum:** density operators are one G4 realization, not base GPTOPT ontology.

**Generalized:** many other convex state spaces are admissible.

**M1:** framework carrier `SOURCE_DERIVED` once a GPT model is declared; the particular state space is `MODEL_CHOICE`/empirically constrained.  
**M2:** `C2–C3` within a chosen model; no unique `C4`.  
**M3:** operational/model-relative carrier; no unique ontology.

### K2 — Redundancy/equivalence

Operational equivalence is near-definitional in the operational framework: preparations/processes with the same probabilities for all allowed tests are identified when the formulation is separated accordingly.

This is not gauge equivalence. It is relative to the physically/mathematically allowed effect/test set, whose exhaustiveness is itself a modeling burden.

**M1:** `SOURCE_DERIVED`/definitional relative to effect set.  
**M2:** `C2`; physical exhaustion not established.  
**M3:** operational equivalence only.

### K3 — Allowed transformations

Physical transformations/events/channels and their sequential/parallel composition are native. Their admissible set is model data.

**Key firewall:** `allowed transformations != actual dynamics`.

Quantum CP maps form one subtheory. Purification can imply reversible dilations only under extra assumptions.

### K4 — Actual dynamics/history selector

Base GPTOPT does not supply one law selecting which allowed process actually occurs. Concrete GPT models may add continuous groups, stochastic processes or Hamiltonian-like dynamics; the quantum subtheory supplies unitary/Hamiltonian dynamics only after that theory is selected.

Result: `NO_BASE_FRAMEWORK_HISTORY_SELECTOR`.

### K5 — Observable/measurement interface

Effects, tests and outcome probabilities are among GPTOPT's strongest native structures. The framework reaches formal and operational semantics, but not automatically calibrated detector realization.

The no-restriction hypothesis is not assumed framework-wide: mathematically positive affine functionals need not all be physically admitted effects.

### K6 — Locality/causality

GPTOPT has no intrinsic Lorentzian spacetime causal structure. Nonsignalling, operational causality, causal ordering and local tomography are distinct concepts and can be optional assumptions.

`NO_SIGNALLING != LORENTZIAN_CAUSALITY`.

### K7 — Scale/RG

No framework-wide renormalization-group or continuum-scale flow is source-bound. Convex mixing, measurement coarse-graining, discarding and information compression are not automatically RG flow.

### K8 — Local-to-global/composition

Composite-system structure is central but underdetermined. Tomographic locality, where assumed, says local measurement statistics determine the global state representation; it does **not** uniquely fix the global state space/composite rule. Minimal, maximal and intermediate composites remain possible in the broad GPT setting.

This is one of FCP-7's strongest source-qualified theory-space results.

### K9 — Physical realization/calibration

Preparations, effects, transformations and probabilities have native laboratory-facing semantics. Mapping them to photons, spins, energies, spacetime regions, detector voltages, cross sections or couplings requires a concrete physical model and calibration chain.

Therefore GPTOPT has **operational semantics without complete framework-wide physical realization**.

### K10 — Empirical discriminator

**K10-A:** no single GPTOPT-wide observable distinguishes the whole meta-framework from quantum theory, because quantum theory is itself an admitted GPT model.

`NO_FRAMEWORK_LEVEL_EMPIRICAL_DISCRIMINATOR_IDENTIFIED`.

**K10-B:** experiments can constrain slices of GPT theory space. The FCP-7 Mazurek et al. source uses theory-agnostic GPT tomography to bound deviations from quantum predictions in a single-photon polarization prepare-and-measure setting. This is G6 evidence against some generalized possibilities in that scope, not proof of global quantum uniqueness.

## 6. Seven major selection burdens

1. **State-space selection:** which convex/operational state space nature realizes.
2. **Effect-set selection:** which mathematically available effects are physically allowed; no-restriction is not automatic.
3. **Composite-system selection:** local spaces do not uniquely fix the global composite state space.
4. **Dynamics selection:** allowed channels do not select actual time evolution.
5. **Causal/tomographic selection:** nonsignalling, operational causality and local tomography are separate optional restrictions.
6. **Reconstruction-principle selection:** quantum-specific theorems require named additional axioms whose physical necessity is not established by the mathematics alone.
7. **Realization/empirical selection:** laboratory semantics do not identify unique particles, scales or calibration, and available experiments constrain only bounded portions of the theory space.

## 7. Framework-level conclusions

- `FW-GPTOPT` remains coherent as one bounded family.
- Quantum theory is a G4 member of the theory space, not a generic consequence of G0–G2.
- Base GPTOPT has no universal dynamics selector.
- Local tomography is optional and insufficient to select a unique composite theory.
- Reconstruction theorems are conditional sufficiency results, not independent predictions of quantum theory.
- A broader operational theory space is scientifically useful because it makes selection burdens explicit and permits theory-agnostic experimental constraints.
- No `KEY_EXTENSION_CANDIDATE` is required.
