# FCP-7 — GPTOPT Reconstruction Axiom Ledger

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Framework:** `FW-GPTOPT`  
**Status:** RECONSTRUCTION-DEPENDENCY CANDIDATE

## 0. Rule

> **Successful reconstruction is a conditional theorem, not an independent prediction of quantum theory.**

Every reconstruction result is attributed to `base GPTOPT + explicitly named additional principles`. No axiom package is back-projected into the base framework, and principles from different reconstruction lineages are not pooled.

## 1. Hardy 2001 lineage

**Source:** `SRC-FCP4-GPT-HARDY-2001`.

Hardy's reconstruction uses five axioms in a broad probabilistic setting. The source itself supplies a particularly strong axiom-removal control: its fifth axiom requires continuous reversible transformations between pure states; removing that axiom's continuity condition yields classical probability theory rather than the target quantum theory.

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Base framework | probabilistic operational state/measurement setting |
| Added quantum-selecting burden | continuous reversible transformation structure among pure states (Hardy Axiom 5, with the other declared axioms retained) |
| Target | finite-dimensional quantum structure in the reconstruction scope |
| Axiom-removal witness | dropping continuity prevents the quantum result and yields the classical alternative in Hardy's analysis |
| Independence/minimality | source shows material dependence; FCP-7 does not infer unique or globally minimal foundations |
| Empirical status | mathematical reconstruction conditional on axioms; no independent prediction of nature's axiom choice |

**FCP disposition:** `Q-A` / `VALID_CONDITIONAL`.

The continuity/reversibility condition does substantial selection work. Its success in reconstructing the known target does not establish that nature selected it independently of the reconstruction goal.

## 2. Purification lineage

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP4-GPT-CDP-PUR-2010`, `SRC-FCP4-OPT-CHIRIBELLA-2014`.

Purification is explicitly an additional principle: every mixed state has a purification unique up to reversible channels on the purifying system under the source's hypotheses. The 2010 source derives strong consequences, including reversible realization/dilation of processes and a state-transformation correspondence with quantum-like structural properties.

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Base framework | operational/probabilistic systems, states, transformations and composition |
| Added principle | purification, plus source-specific ancillary hypotheses where required |
| Derived structure | reversible dilation of physical processes; strong information-processing consequences; operational GNS/Stinespring-like structure in later synthesis |
| Does purification alone uniquely select complex QM? | **not established by the bounded sources** |
| Empirical status | no framework-level empirical selection of purification is bound in FCP-7 |

**FCP disposition:** `Q-A` / `VALID_CONDITIONAL`.

Purification is scientifically substantive but cannot be counted as a base GPTOPT consequence or as unique evidence for quantum theory.

## 4. Local tomography is not a unique-composite theorem

The same Müller source supplies an important selection control. Even if Tomographic Locality is assumed, two local state spaces generally admit many inequivalent global composites. In finite-dimensional GPT language, tomographically local composite state spaces can lie between minimal and maximal tensor products.

Therefore:

> **Tomographic Locality does not select the quantum tensor product/state space by itself.**

This is not merely an absence of proof; it is an explicit theory-space nonuniqueness within the bounded framework.

**FCP disposition:** `COUNTERMODELED` against any stronger claim that local tomography alone fixes global quantum composition.

## 5. Barrett 2007 post-quantum witness

**Source:** `SRC-FCP4-GPT-BARRETT-2007`.

Barrett's framework contains classical and quantum theories and also well-defined nonquantum models, including a theory with arbitrary nonsignalling correlations such as PR-box correlations stronger than quantum CHSH correlations.

This provides a reusable negative witness:

> **Basic probabilistic/operational consistency plus nonsignalling does not uniquely select quantum correlations.**

Additional structure is required to explain why the physically realized correlation set appears quantum rather than the full nonsignalling set.

## 6. Axiom-pooling prohibition

The following inference is prohibited:

`Hardy continuity/reversibility`
`+ CDP purification`
`+ Müller local tomography/subspace package`
`= base GPTOPT`.

These belong to distinct source lineages and theorem packages.

Likewise, if several different axiom sets reconstruct the same quantum target, FCP treats that as evidence that the target can be characterized in multiple ways—not as evidence that any one axiom package is uniquely fundamental.

## 7. Retrospective-selection assessment

The bounded reconstruction sources explicitly target the already-known quantum formalism. FCP-7 therefore records a retrospective-selection burden for foundational interpretation:

1. mathematical sufficiency can be established;
2. physical motivation of individual axioms can be argued;
3. independent empirical selection of the complete axiom package is not established by reconstruction alone;
4. uniqueness/minimality of one package is not established at the bounded source scope.

No numerical ranking among reconstruction programs is produced.

## 8. Main selection result

The source-bound reconstruction record supports:

> **QUANTUM THEORY IS NOT FORCED BY THE BASE GPTOPT OPERATIONAL SKELETON.**

and:

> **ADDITIONAL PRINCIPLES DO REAL MATHEMATICAL SELECTION WORK, BUT FCP-7 DOES NOT ESTABLISH THAT NATURE UNIQUELY SELECTS ANY ONE RECONSTRUCTION PACKAGE.**
