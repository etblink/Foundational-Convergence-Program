## 0. Purpose

This packet freezes the deliberately small FCP-8 source layer used to study the boundary between ordinary quantum correlations and the wider GPT/operational possibility space. It does not compare GPTOPT with Reduced NFC, AQFT, CQM, causal sets, or any other foundational framework.

The controlling distinction is:

> **Mathematical GPT possibility, quantum admissibility, theoretical-principle exclusion, and empirical exclusion are different evidentiary categories.**

The FCP-7 `G0`–`G6` layers remain controlling. FCP-8 primarily adds source material at `G5` (post-quantum/generalized theory space) and `G6` (empirical restriction).

## 1. FCP-8 theory-space vocabulary

- `GPT-ALLOWED` — mathematically admissible inside the declared GPT/operational model.
- `QUANTUM-ALLOWED` — belongs to the ordinary quantum set at the declared scope.
- `POSTQUANTUM-ALLOWED` — GPT/nonsignalling admissible but outside the quantum set.
- `EMPIRICALLY-CONSTRAINED` — experiment places a quantitative bound on a generalized region.
- `EMPIRICALLY-EXCLUDED` — a declared region/model is incompatible with qualified data under stated assumptions.
- `PRINCIPLE-EXCLUDED` — a named theoretical principle excludes the region/model.
- `UNTESTED` — no adequate direct empirical constraint is source-bound.
- `OPEN-SELECTION` — more than one source-qualified theory/principle remains viable.

These labels describe different dimensions and may co-occur when the dimension is stated.

## 2. New FCP-8 theoretical sources

### `SRC-FCP8-GPT-PR-1994`
Sandu Popescu and Daniel Rohrlich, **Quantum nonlocality as an axiom**, *Foundations of Physics* 24, 379–385 (1994). DOI `10.1007/BF02058098`.

**Role:** foundational post-quantum/nonsignalling witness. The paper exhibits superquantum correlations that preserve relativistic no-signalling while violating CHSH more strongly than quantum correlations.

**Scope ceiling:** mathematical/operational possibility. It is not evidence that PR-box correlations occur in nature.

### `SRC-FCP8-GPT-TSIRELSON-1980`
B. S. Cirel'son (Tsirelson), **Quantum generalizations of Bell's inequality**, *Letters in Mathematical Physics* 4, 93–100 (1980). DOI `10.1007/BF00417500`.

**Role:** foundational quantum-bound source establishing quantum Bell-correlation restrictions stricter than the algebraic/nonsignalling maximum in the relevant scenario.

**Scope ceiling:** quantum-theory theorem; not a physical explanation of why nature selects the quantum bound.

### `SRC-FCP8-GPT-IC-2009`
Marcin Pawłowski, Tomasz Paterek, Dagomir Kaszlikowski, Valerio Scarani, Andreas Winter and Marek Żukowski, **Information causality as a physical principle**, *Nature* 461, 1101–1104 (2009). DOI `10.1038/nature08400`; arXiv `0905.2292`.

**Role:** primary quantum-boundary principle. Information Causality bounds information gain by communicated classical information, is obeyed by classical/quantum theory in the source, and excludes important stronger-than-quantum nonsignalling correlations.

**Scope ceiling:** principled restriction, not a proof that Information Causality uniquely characterizes the full quantum correlation set.

### `SRC-FCP8-GPT-ML-2010`
Miguel Navascués and Harald Wunderlich, **A glance beyond the quantum model**, *Proceedings of the Royal Society A* 466, 881–890 (2010; online 2009). DOI `10.1098/rspa.2009.0453`; arXiv `0907.0372`.

**Role:** primary Macroscopic Locality source. It imposes recovery of classical locality in a macroscopic limit and derives nontrivial correlation restrictions while still permitting post-quantum correlations.

**Scope ceiling:** principle-defined superset/restriction; not unique recovery of quantum theory.

### `SRC-FCP8-GPT-AQ-2015`
Miguel Navascués, Yelena Guryanova, Matty J. Hoban and Antonio Acín, **Almost quantum correlations**, *Nature Communications* 6, 6288 (2015). DOI `10.1038/ncomms7288`; arXiv `1403.4621`.

**Role:** primary countermodel set for principle sufficiency. The `almost quantum` set strictly contains the quantum set while satisfying many proposed quantum-correlation principles; the paper reports numerical evidence concerning Information Causality.

**Scope ceiling:** post-quantum correlation set/countermodel. It is not established as physically realized.

### `SRC-FCP8-GPT-IC-2026`
Baichu Yu and Valerio Scarani, **Information causality beyond the random-access-code model**, *Physical Review A* 114, 012202 (2026). DOI `10.1103/s52j-2jr7`.

**Role:** current-status primary source. It strengthens Information Causality analysis and explicitly reports that some correlation families still exhibit a gap between the IC-derived boundary and the quantum set.

**Scope ceiling:** current progress on one principle; no full quantum-set characterization established.

## 3. New FCP-8 empirical source

### `SRC-FCP8-GPT-RINGBAUER-2014`
Martin Ringbauer, Alessandro Fedrizzi, Dominic W. Berry and Andrew G. White, **Information Causality in the Quantum and Post-Quantum Regime**, *Scientific Reports* 4, 6955 (2014). DOI `10.1038/srep06955`.

**Role:** experimental implementation of the Information Causality task across classical/quantum and *emulated* superquantum correlations.

**Critical scope control:** the beyond-quantum correlations are produced by polarization-dependent loss and postselection inside quantum optics. The experiment therefore demonstrates the operational IC test and its response to emulated supraquantum statistics; it is not an observation of a naturally realized post-quantum theory and does not empirically select IC as a law of nature.

**Evidence class:** `E3` principle-test methodology, not `E4` global framework selection.

## 5. Bounded Bell-correlation nesting

At the finite Bell-scenario scope supported by the source set, FCP-8 records the strict logical hierarchy:

`classical/local correlations` ⊂ `quantum correlations` ⊂ `nonsignalling correlations`.

The PR-box is a witness to the second strict inclusion. In conventional CHSH normalization:

- local hidden-variable bound: `2`;
- quantum Tsirelson bound: `2√2`;
- PR/algebraic maximum: `4`.

This hierarchy is a statement about declared correlation sets, not about the entire GPTOPT framework.

## 6. Largest theory spaces admitted in FCP-8

FCP-8 does **not** define one largest universal GPTOPT theory space.

Two bounded outer envelopes are used for different questions:

1. **Bell-correlation envelope:** the full nonsignalling set for the declared finite input/output scenario.
2. **Composite-system envelope:** the maximal GPT tensor/composite construction for fixed local state spaces, as source-bound already in FCP-4/FCP-7 materials.

They are not identified with one another and neither is claimed to contain every logically possible foundational theory.

## 7. Quantum-boundary principle status

### No-signalling

Necessary for the bounded relativistic operational setting but radically insufficient to select quantum theory: PR-box correlations satisfy no-signalling while exceeding quantum CHSH bounds.

### Information Causality

Substantially stronger than no-signalling and able to recover important quantum boundaries including the CHSH/Tsirelson point in the original analysis. It does not currently provide a complete source-qualified characterization of the entire quantum correlation set. The 2026 source still reports gaps for some families.

### Macroscopic Locality

Physically motivated by recovery of locality in a macroscopic limit and able to recover significant quantum constraints, but its admitted set is larger than the quantum set.

**FCP-8 status:** `QUANTUM_BOUNDARY_SELECTION_OPEN` and `QUANTUM_BOUNDARY_PRINCIPLE_UNDERDETERMINATION` at this bounded source scope.

## 8. Empirical evidence hierarchy

- `E0` — quantum-assuming fit.
- `E1` — model-independent operational constraint.
- `E2` — direct exclusion/bounding of a defined post-quantum alternative region under stated assumptions.
- `E3` — laboratory test/implementation of a proposed selecting principle.
- `E4` — global unique selection of quantum theory from the relevant broad comparator space.

FCP-8 binds:

- Mazurek 2021: `E1`, reaching bounded `L2` because sufficiently large deviations from the qubit/GPT geometry and associated supraquantum CHSH capability are quantitatively bounded under the tomography-completeness assumption;
- Ringbauer 2014: `E3` methodology only, because supraquantum correlations are emulated by postselection rather than observed as natural post-quantum physics.

No `E4` source is identified.

## 9. Empirical-selection ladder

- `L0` — compatibility;
- `L1` — classical alternatives excluded;
- `L2` — specific post-quantum alternatives/regions bounded or rejected;
- `L3` — broad generalized theory space strongly narrowed;
- `L4` — quantum theory uniquely selected from the relevant broad comparator space.

**Highest FCP-8 level:** `L2`.

The project does not promote bounded low-dimensional GPT tomography or Bell-correlation tests to `L3`/`L4`.
