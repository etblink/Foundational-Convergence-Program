**Version:** 0.1.0

**Status:** BOUNDED SUPPLEMENTAL SOURCE RECORD CANDIDATE

## Why a supplement is required

The frozen FCP-4 GPTOPT corpus is sufficient for the operational framework, reconstruction, composition and post-quantum theory-space questions, but FCP-4 intentionally contains **zero direct framework-level empirical source records**.

FCP-7 K10-B explicitly asks whether experimental data can constrain generalized-probabilistic theory space without assuming the quantum formalism. Answering that question requires one minimal empirical supplement.

No other FCP-7 source expansion is authorized by this artifact.

## `SRC-FCP7-GPT-MAZUREK-2021`

**Authors:** Michael D. Mazurek, Matthew F. Pusey, Kevin J. Resch, Robert W. Spekkens  
**Title:** *Experimentally Bounding Deviations From Quantum Theory in the Landscape of Generalized Probabilistic Theories*  
**Journal:** PRX Quantum **2**, 020302 (2021)  
**DOI:** `10.1103/PRXQuantum.2.020302`  
**arXiv:** `1710.05948`  
**Authority type:** primary empirical/methodological source  
**FCP layer:** `G6 — empirical/physical restriction`  
**Status:** `SOURCE_BOUND`

### Source role

The work develops generalized-probabilistic-theory tomography that simultaneously characterizes preparations and measurements without presuming quantum state/measurement tomography, then applies it to a large prepare-and-measure data set for single-photon polarization.

The source reports, within its assumptions and data:

- the most plausible GPT vector-space dimension is 4, matching the qubit prediction;
- experimentally compatible state/effect polytopes closely approximate the Bloch-sphere description;
- quantitative bounds can be placed on how much stronger relevant nonlocal/contextual correlations could be than the quantum prediction in the tested system.

### Important limitation

The source explicitly identifies tomographic completeness of the implemented preparation/measurement set as an important loophole/assumption burden. The experiment concerns one bounded physical degree of freedom and does not test all GPT composites, dynamics, reconstruction axioms, or post-quantum possibilities.
