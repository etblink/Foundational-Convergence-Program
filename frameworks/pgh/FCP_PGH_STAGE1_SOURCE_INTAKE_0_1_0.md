## 4. External primary corpus

The external corpus is deliberately compact. Its purpose is not to collect votes on PGH; it establishes prior art, generic causal/network mathematics, membership-testing machinery, physical counterboundaries, and the current minimal triangle landscape.

### `SRC-PGH-EXT-01-FRITZ-CORRELATION-2012`

**Tobias Fritz**, *Beyond Bell's Theorem: Correlation Scenarios*, New Journal of Physics 14, 103001 (2012), arXiv:`1206.5115`.

- Primary role: `FORMAL_MODEL_FOUNDATION`.
- Use: establishes independent-source correlation scenarios including triangle-like networks without measurement-input choices as pre-PGH causal/network mathematics.
- Ceiling: does not assert PGH's universal physical law.

### `SRC-PGH-EXT-02-FRITZ-CAUSAL-2014`

**Tobias Fritz**, *Beyond Bell's Theorem II: Scenarios with arbitrary causal structure*, arXiv:`1404.4812` (later Communications in Mathematical Physics).

- Primary role: `CAUSAL_OR_LATENT_MODEL_TAXONOMY`.
- Use: places classical/quantum/general correlations on arbitrary DAGs and explicitly relates the formalism to causal inference with latent variables/hidden Bayesian networks.
- Ceiling: generic causal-model architecture, not PGH-specific physical interpretation.

### `SRC-PGH-EXT-03-HENSON-LAL-PUSEY-2014`

**Joe Henson, Raymond Lal, Matthew F. Pusey**, *Theory-independent limits on correlations from generalised Bayesian networks*, New Journal of Physics 16, 113043 (2014), arXiv:`1405.2572`.

- Primary role: `FORMAL_NONTRIVIALITY_OR_EXCLUSION`.
- Use: proves theory-independent constraints can exceed observable conditional independences; in particular, perfect three-party correlation cannot be produced using only bipartite common causes in the generalized-network setting.
- Ceiling: strongly shows that common-bit-type exclusion is not uniquely PGH mathematics or uniquely classical; it does not establish PGH's universal physical scope.

### `SRC-PGH-EXT-04-WOLFE-SPEKKENS-FRITZ-INFLATION-2016`

## 5. Current external-source synthesis

The external corpus establishes four facts relevant to taxonomy readiness.

### 5.1 The triangle independent-source model is prior art

Independent pairwise-source networks, triangle correlation scenarios, latent-variable DAG compatibility, and their observable restrictions substantially predate PGH.

```text
TRIANGLE_NETWORK_MODEL_ORIGIN = EXTERNAL_PRIOR_ART
PGH_NOVELTY_CREDIT_FOR_TRIANGLE_FORMALISM = NONE
PGH_NOVELTY_CREDIT_FOR_GENERIC_MEMBERSHIP_TESTING = NONE
```

### 5.2 Proper-class exclusion is not uniquely PGH

Generalized Bayesian-network results and inflation methods establish nontrivial restrictions independently. The broad logical form “pairwise independent/common causes cannot generate every three-party distribution” is established prior art.

### 5.3 External physics does not privilege the classical triangle class universally

Quantum-network literature supplies theoretical and experimental counterboundaries in which independent physical sources generate correlations not representable by classical triangle-local models. Recent work reaches the no-input, binary-output triangle itself.

This does **not** adjudicate PGH against a target because PGH's target eligibility, chronology, discovery contamination rules, statistical decision rule, and exact observed data are separate burdens.

It does establish that PGH's universal physical claim is a strong physical postulate with nontrivial tension against known quantum-network possibilities, not a harmless restatement of generic causal mathematics.

### 5.4 The candidate-specific content is primarily the universal physical restriction

At Stage-1 scope, the most PGH-specific content is therefore not the mathematical existence of `T_ind`, but the proposition:

```text
FOR_ALL_I_ELIGIBLE_PHYSICAL_RECORD_INTERFACES:
    p_emp in T_ind
```

That proposition is explicit, falsifiable, and proposal-defining, but is not independently established by the external corpus.
