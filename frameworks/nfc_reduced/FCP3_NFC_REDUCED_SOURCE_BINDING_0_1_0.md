\[

\[
\Sigma_{\mathcal T}=\Omega/\!\sim_{\mathcal T}.
\]

**Assumptions:** supplied carrier and test family; well-defined test outcomes.  
**Provenance:** reduction Results 1, 12, 13; reduced handoff `K_red`.
