# FCP-20 — AS / Null-GR Nonforcing Countermodels

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** COUNTERMODEL CANDIDATE  
**Frameworks:** `FW-AS`, `FW-NULL-GRQFTSM`

## 0. Purpose

These witnesses test whether apparently strong AS/null-GR conclusions follow from the frozen packet. They are negative controls, not refutations of `FW-AS`.

## CM-01 — exact FRG equation without exact quantum gravity solution

Take an exact-form functional flow equation and project it onto a finite gravitational truncation. The formal flow equation may be exact while the truncation solution is approximate.

Defeats:

`EXACT_FRG_EQUATION -> EXACT_COMPLETE_AS_QUANTUM_GRAVITY`.

Preserves:

formal `AS-L1` structure and truncation evidence.

## CM-02 — fixed point in one truncation without complete-theory fixed point

A finite-dimensional truncation exhibits a non-Gaussian fixed point while omitted operators remain uncontrolled.

Defeats:

`FIXED_POINT_IN_ONE_TRUNCATION -> COMPLETE_THEORY_FIXED_POINT`.

Preserves:

`AS-L2` evidence.

## CM-03 — repeated truncation fixed points without theorem

Many increasingly rich truncations show a persistent fixed-point pattern, yet no construction controls the complete gravitational theory space.

Defeats:

`MULTI_TRUNCATION_PERSISTENCE -> EXACT_EXISTENCE_THEOREM`.

Preserves:

`AS-L3_MULTI_TRUNCATION_ROBUSTNESS`.

## CM-04 — finite relevant directions in truncations without exact critical-surface dimension

Two admissible truncation/parametrization choices yield different small relevant-direction counts while both display interacting fixed points.

Defeats:

`SMALL_TRUNCATION_COUNTS -> EXACT_PHYSICAL_CRITICAL_SURFACE_INTEGER`.

Preserves:

conditional finite-critical-surface predictivity architecture.

## CM-05 — RG flow without physical time evolution

A scale-dependent effective action changes with RG scale `k` while describing no unique temporal sequence of spacetime events.

Defeats:

`RG_FLOW -> PHYSICAL_HISTORY_SELECTOR`.

Preserves:

K3 scale transformation structure.

## CM-06 — UV fixed point without unique physical UV→IR trajectory

A finite-dimensional UV critical surface can contain multiple relevant trajectories distinguished by free relevant coordinates and low-energy matching.

Defeats:

`UV_FIXED_POINT -> UNIQUE_REALISTIC_HISTORY`.

Preserves:

AS-H and the finite-parameter predictivity logic.

## CM-07 — GR-like infrared regime without independent empirical rediscovery

Select/calibrate an AS trajectory so its infrared effective action agrees with tested GR in the domain used for matching.

Defeats:

`RECOVERED_GR_SUCCESS -> INDEPENDENT_AS_EMPIRICAL_EVIDENCE`.

The successful GR observations are evidence for the recovered target and compatibility of the selected AS realization, but are not automatically independent AS E4 evidence.

## CM-08 — Standard-Model-compatible fixed point without SM derivation

A gravity–matter truncation admits the observed SM field content while alternative matter contents or implementations remain possible.

Defeats:

`SM_COMPATIBILITY -> STANDARD_MODEL_DERIVATION`.

Preserves:

gravity–matter compatibility evidence.

## CM-09 — Lorentzian truncation result without complete Lorentzian quantum gravity

A foliated Lorentzian Einstein-Hilbert or two-point truncation exhibits compatible fixed-point structure while full theory-space unitarity and causal observables remain unconstructed.

Defeats:

`LORENTZIAN_FIXED_POINT_EVIDENCE -> COMPLETE_UNITARY_LORENTZIAN_QG`.

Preserves:

nonempty material Lorentzian progress.

## CM-10 — RG-improved black hole without compulsory AS prediction

Choose an RG scale-identification prescription and improve a classical black-hole geometry. Another admissible prescription changes the geometry.

Defeats:

`RG_IMPROVED_BLACK_HOLE -> UNAVOIDABLE_BASE_AS_PREDICTION`.

Preserves:

optional phenomenology as a legitimate model program.

## CM-11 — shared RG mathematics without independent convergence

Apply Wilsonian RG/fixed-point machinery to an ordinary non-gravitational QFT. The same broad mathematical structures appear without the AS gravitational UV-completion hypothesis.

Defeats:

`SHARED_RG_LANGUAGE -> INDEPENDENT_FOUNDATIONAL_CONVERGENCE`.

Preserves:

the possibility that a specific gravitational fixed-point instantiation is AS-specific.

## CM-12 — null UV incompleteness without positive AS evidence

The null GR+QFT baseline lacks an established UV-complete quantum-gravity law. Multiple mutually incompatible UV-completion hypotheses could fill that open slot.

Defeats:

`NULL_QG_OPEN_PROBLEM -> POSITIVE_EVIDENCE_FOR_AS`.

## CM-13 — Einstein-Hilbert truncation without E1 framework equivalence

An AS truncation contains an Einstein-Hilbert term identical in algebraic form to the classical GR action, but the AS framework-level object additionally includes RG theory space, fixed-point conditions and trajectory structure.

Defeats:

`SHARED_EINSTEIN_HILBERT_SUBTERM -> E1_WHOLE_KEY_OR_FRAMEWORK_EQUIVALENCE`.

Preserves:

a real lineage relation.

## CM-14 — GR-like trajectory without FCP-2 E3 record

A source describes a trajectory as approaching a classical/GR-like infrared regime but the frozen FCP packet does not provide one complete record of target quantities, convergence/error, validity domain and calibration preservation.

Defeats:

`VERBAL_OR_PARTIAL_GR_RECOVERY -> AUTOMATIC_E3`.

Preserves:

the scientific reality of the trajectory result and the possibility that a future source-strengthening phase could qualify an E3 subrelation.

## 1. Countermodel conclusions

The witnesses collectively force these controls:

- AS-specific residue can survive without pairwise convergence.
- lineage relations can be real without independent credit.
- robustness evidence can be substantial without exactification.
- target recovery can be scientifically valuable without becoming independent rediscovery.
- open null problems cannot select AS.
- optional phenomenology cannot bind the whole framework.

No countermodel in this packet falsifies the Asymptotic Safety hypothesis. Their role is to prevent claims from exceeding their frozen provenance and physical bridges.
