# FCP-12 — Reduced NFC / CST Countermodels and Anti-Smuggling Witnesses

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Frameworks:** `FW-NFC-RED`, `FW-CST`  
**Status:** NEGATIVE-WITNESS CANDIDATE

## C1 — Generic relational-container witness

A generic relational/process category can contain partial orders, graphs, automata, lattices and many other inequivalent structures.

Defeats:

> Reduced NFC has a relational carrier and CST has a relational carrier, therefore the two independently converge.

Does not defeat a future source-bound specialization with an exact E1/E2 map.

## C2 — Quotient witness

Observational indistinguishability under a selected test family and order-isomorphism under relabelling are different equivalence predicates. Generic theories frequently quotient labels, gauges, representations or observationally indistinguishable states.

Defeats distinctive convergence credit for K2 absent an explicit map.

## C3 — Finite-description witness

Finite carriers, finite alphabets and finite-capacity bounds occur throughout mathematics and computation without any claim that spacetime is fundamentally discrete.

Defeats:

> Reduced NFC uses finite descriptions, therefore it independently supports CST fundamental spacetime discreteness.

## C4 — Interface/causality witness

A finite interface or boundary statistic can exist in graph, automata, coding, PDE discretization or other models without Lorentzian causal semantics, transitive causal order or CST local finiteness.

Defeats:

> NFC interface structure and CST causal order are the same physical structure.

## C5 — Local/global genericity witness

Sheaf gluing, bundle patching, PDE boundary-value problems, graph extension, constraint satisfaction, category-theoretic colimits and many other frameworks have local-to-global consistency questions.

Defeats:

> Reduced-NFC Globalization and CST Hauptvermutung/manifold reconstruction converge merely because both are local/global problems.

## C6 — Shared no-dynamics witness

Two frameworks may lack a unique dynamics for unrelated reasons. Reduced NFC lacks a source-selected physical law at its reduced spine; minimal CST is kinematic but optional CSG/QSG dynamics programs exist.

Defeats:

> Both lack one universal core dynamics, therefore they converge on K4.

## C7 — Target-conditioned reconstruction witness

A causal set produced by sprinkling into a selected Lorentzian continuum is manifoldlike relative to that target by construction. FCP-11 already classifies this as target-conditioned realization/reconstruction.

Defeats laundering a Reduced-NFC/CST match through supplied continuum structure as independent emergence.

## C8 — Post-hoc realization witness

Because Reduced NFC intentionally leaves `C`, `T`, physical realization and many process choices open, one can retrospectively choose interpretations that make it resemble many frameworks.

Defeats:

> Let an NFC configuration be a causal set; therefore NFC independently predicted CST.

Only an independently source-bound mapping could overcome this witness.

## C9 — FIS false-positive witness

The existence of finite intervals, stems, boundaries or local order data in CST does not imply that a finite statistic `c` is sufficient for a selected exterior/global outcome `q` in the exact sense `q = Phi ∘ c`.

A system can possess abundant finite local data while global outcomes depend on additional degrees of freedom.

Defeats FIS inference from locality vocabulary or finite cardinality alone.

## C10 — Viability/manifold-selection witness

Defining a subset of manifoldlike causal sets does not show it is invariant, attractive or high-probability under a selected CST dynamics. FCP-9's generic-poset and dynamics countermodels already separate kinematic definition from dynamical selection.

Defeats:

> Manifoldlikeness is a viability condition, therefore Reduced-NFC viability has been instantiated physically in CST.

A genuine viability correspondence would require a selected dynamics and an invariance/attraction theorem.

## C11 — Universal-completion/Hauptvermutung witness

A generic colimit or universal object, when it exists in a selected category, does not solve CST's physical continuum uniqueness problem. The two constructions can differ in objects, morphisms, uniqueness equivalence and physical interpretation.

Defeats identification of R10 with the Hauptvermutung by the word `completion` alone.

## C12 — Empirical-absence witness

Two frameworks lacking a pairwise empirical discriminator do not thereby make the same predictions or share empirical content.

Defeats:

> Neither Reduced NFC nor CST has a framework-level pairwise prediction, therefore they are empirically convergent.

## Summary controls

These witnesses preserve the FCP-12 firewalls:

> **GENERIC RELATIONAL MATHEMATICS != FOUNDATIONAL CONVERGENCE.**

> **FINITE DESCRIPTION != FUNDAMENTAL SPACETIME DISCRETENESS.**

> **OBSERVATIONAL QUOTIENT != CAUSAL-SET ORDER ISOMORPHISM.**

> **INTERFACE STRUCTURE != PHYSICAL CAUSAL ORDER.**

> **LOCAL-TO-GLOBAL QUESTION != SHARED GLOBALIZATION THEOREM.**

> **SHARED UNDERDETERMINATION != CONVERGENCE.**

> **TARGET-CONDITIONED CST RECONSTRUCTION != INDEPENDENT CST EMERGENCE.**

> **NO CST PHYSICAL SEMANTICS MAY BE BACK-PROJECTED INTO REDUCED NFC WITHOUT A SOURCE-BOUND MAP.**
