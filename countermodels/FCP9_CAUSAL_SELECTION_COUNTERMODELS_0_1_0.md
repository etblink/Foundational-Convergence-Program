## C1 — Generic-poset witness against automatic manifoldlikeness

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-KR-1975`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-SURYA-2019`.

The set of finite partial orders is asymptotically dominated by highly non-manifoldlike order types rather than low-dimensional Lorentzian-manifold approximants.

Defeats:

> locally finite/finite partial order + counting automatically yields realistic spacetime.

Does **not** defeat:

- a dynamics that strongly suppresses such orders;
- restricted sample spaces;
- the existence of manifoldlike causal sets.

Status: `COUNTERMODELED` for kinematic inevitability of manifoldlikeness.

## C8 — Embedding witness against continuum emergence by assumption

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-BLMS-1987`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-SURYA-2019`.

- mathematical possibility;

- dynamics;
- phenomenological model choice;
- empirical selection.

They carry no cross-framework convergence claim.
