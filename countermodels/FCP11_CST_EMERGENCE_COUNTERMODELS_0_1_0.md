# FCP-11 — CST / Null-GR Emergence-Control Countermodels

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Frameworks:** `FW-CST`, `FW-NULL-GRQFTSM`  
**Status:** NEGATIVE-WITNESS / SUBTRACTION-CONTROL CANDIDATE

## 0. Purpose

These witnesses defeat specific overclaims that can arise when continuum reconstruction is confused with emergence or when an open problem in the null baseline is treated as evidence for CST.

They preserve all positive source-qualified CST results at their exact scope.

## C1 — Sprinkled-manifold witness against emergence by construction

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-BLMS-1987`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-SURYA-2019`.

A causal set produced by Poisson sprinkling into a chosen Lorentzian manifold is manifoldlike relative to that chosen continuum by construction.

Defeats:

> successful recovery of continuum observables on a sprinkled causal set proves that unrestricted CST dynamics generates manifoldlike spacetime.

Does not defeat:

- faithful-embedding/sprinkling as a valid continuum approximation;
- quantitative reconstruction tests on manifoldlike causal sets;
- a future independent dynamics theorem selecting the manifoldlike sector.

FCP-11 classification: `COUNTERMODELED` for emergence-by-input.

## C2 — Generic-poset witness against automatic GR configuration space

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-KR-1975`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-SURYA-2019`.

Generic large finite partial orders are dominated by highly nonmanifoldlike structures rather than low-dimensional Lorentzian-manifold approximants.

Defeats:

> locally finite causal-order kinematics alone selects the GR-like sector.

Does not imply that a physically selected CST dynamics must sample causal sets uniformly or generically.

FCP-11 use: blocks any identification of the full CST carrier with the GR configuration space.

## C3 — CSG-family witness against unique GR-generating dynamics

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-RS-2000`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-SURYA-2019`.

The classical sequential-growth assumptions constrain a broad stochastic family with free coupling data.

Defeats:

> covariance/causality/growth conditions uniquely determine the CST law or already select Einstein-like histories.

Preserves:

- CSG as a mathematically controlled D2 family;
- the possibility of additional principles selecting a member or a different dynamics.

## C4 — QSG-current-status witness against completed quantum emergence

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-SZ-2020`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-SS-2026`.

Quantum/complex sequential-growth work establishes nontrivial covariance, measure-extension and transition-algebra constraints while the general quantum transition-operator problem remains unsolved at the bounded source scope.

Defeats:

> CST already possesses a selected first-principles quantum dynamics from which GR emerges.

Does not establish impossibility of a future solution.

## C5 — Continuum operator/action witness against K4 promotion

**Source:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-BD-2010`.

A causal-set d'Alembertian/curvature/action can approach the corresponding continuum structures under manifoldlike and field/scale assumptions.

Defeats the invalid implication:

> one successful discrete-to-continuum action/operator limit implies that the framework's actual quantum histories are selected by that action and reproduce Einstein dynamics.

Preserves:

- the selected `E3_CONTROLLED_LIMIT` subclaim where the source conditions are met;
- the mathematical/physical importance of the causal-set construction.

## C6 — BHS background witness against Lorentz-emergence overclaim

**Source:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-BHS-2009`.

The no-preferred-direction theorem starts with Poisson sprinklings into Minkowski spacetime.

Defeats:

> the theorem derives Lorentzian spacetime or Lorentz symmetry from an arbitrary causal set.

Preserves the actual theorem:

> CST-type Poisson discreteness in the declared Minkowski setting need not introduce an intrinsically extractable preferred inertial frame.

## C7 — Matter-extension witness against R5 recovery

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-SURYA-2019`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-SURYA-2025`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-BD-2010`.

Fields/operators can be added to causal sets and can possess meaningful continuum limits, while Standard Model matter is not part of minimal C0 CST and is not source-selected by the bounded dynamics corpus.

Defeats:

> recovery of continuum geometry automatically recovers realistic matter, parameters and calibration.

FCP-11 consequence: K9 remains partial; framework-wide R5 is not established.

## C8 — Phenomenology witness against framework-level empirical selection

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-LAMBDA-2004`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-ZUNTZ-2008`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-PDS-2009`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-CDP-2010`.

Causal-set-inspired models can produce empirically constrainable departures from ordinary null phenomenology, but their extra stochastic/unimodular/diffusion assumptions are not forced by canonical C0 CST.

Defeats both overclaims:

1. a constrained/disfavored optional model falsifies all CST;
2. the mere existence of testable optional models empirically selects CST.

Framework status remains:

`NO_FRAMEWORK_LEVEL_EMPIRICAL_DISCRIMINATOR_IDENTIFIED`.

## C9 — Null-gap witness against argument from incompleteness

**Sources:** FCP-1/FCP-2 null baseline; FCP-9/FCP-10 CST binding.

The bounded GR+QFT/SM null competitor lacks one established UV-complete quantum-gravity dynamics.

Defeats:

> because the null baseline is incomplete at the quantum-gravity frontier, CST is thereby supported or selected.

Multiple inequivalent quantum-gravity programs can address the same open frontier; positive CST evidence must come from CST-specific derivation/realization/experiment.

## C10 — Generic-redundancy witness against diffeomorphism identification

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP9-CST-RS-2000`, `SRC-FCP9-CST-OBS-2003`, `SRC-FCP2-NULL-DECOMP-001`.

Relabelling invariance, gauge redundancy and diffeomorphism redundancy all instantiate the broad mathematical idea that descriptive labels need not be physical.

Defeats:

> because both CST and GR quotient representational labels, their physical equivalence structures are the same.

FCP-11 ceiling: `E5_FUNCTIONAL_ANALOGY` absent a stronger source-bound map.

## Summary

The negative witnesses enforce six permanent FCP-11 separations:

> **TARGET SUPPLIED != TARGET EMERGED.**

> **CONTINUUM RECONSTRUCTION != DYNAMICAL MANIFOLD SELECTION.**

> **CONTROLLED ACTION/OPERATOR LIMIT != COMPLETE GR DYNAMICS.**

> **LORENTZ COMPATIBILITY != LORENTZ-GEOMETRY DERIVATION.**

> **OPTIONAL PHENOMENOLOGY != FRAMEWORK-LEVEL EMPIRICAL SELECTION.**

> **NULL INCOMPLETENESS != POSITIVE CST EVIDENCE.**

These countermodels do not erase CST results; they delimit what those results establish.

> **Preserve results, not theories.**
