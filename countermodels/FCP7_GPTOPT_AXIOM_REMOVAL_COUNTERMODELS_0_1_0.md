# FCP-7 — GPTOPT Axiom-Removal and Theory-Space Countermodels

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** COUNTERMODEL LEDGER CANDIDATE  
**Framework:** `FW-GPTOPT`

## Purpose

This artifact preserves reusable negative witnesses showing that broad operational/probabilistic structure does not uniquely force the quantum theory. The witnesses remain source-bounded and are not generalized beyond their stated assumptions.

## CM1 — Hardy continuity-removal witness

**Source:** `SRC-FCP4-GPT-HARDY-2001`.

Hardy's reconstruction explicitly identifies the continuity/reversible-transformation postulate as quantum-selecting. With the other declared axioms retained, dropping the continuity requirement yields classical probability theory instead of the quantum target.

**Blocks:**

> `base probabilistic framework -> quantum theory` without the quantum-selecting axiom.

**Classification:** `COUNTERMODELED` against inevitability from the weaker axiom set.

**Scope ceiling:** Hardy 2001 reconstruction only; not a theorem about every later reconstruction.

## CM2 — Tomographic-locality composite nonuniqueness

**Source:** `SRC-FCP4-GPT-MULLER-2021`.

Even when Tomographic Locality is imposed, fixed local state spaces do not uniquely select a global composite state space. The bounded synthesis permits a range of tomographically local composites between minimal and maximal tensor products.

**Blocks:**

> `local tomography + local state spaces -> unique quantum composite`.

**Classification:** `COUNTERMODELED`.

**Scope ceiling:** finite-dimensional GPT composite setting of the source.

## CM3 — Nonsignalling post-quantum correlation witness

**Source:** `SRC-FCP4-GPT-BARRETT-2007`.

Barrett constructs a GPT with well-defined states/dynamics admitting arbitrary nonsignalling correlations including PR-box-type correlations stronger than quantum CHSH correlations.

**Blocks:**

> `operational probability + nonsignalling -> quantum correlation set`.

**Classification:** `COUNTERMODELED`.

**Scope ceiling:** nonsignalling/correlation selection; does not imply PR-box physics is realized.

## CM4 — Purification conditionality witness

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP4-GPT-CDP-PUR-2010`, `SRC-FCP4-OPT-CHIRIBELLA-2014`.

The purification consequences are explicitly theorems of probabilistic theories **with purification** (plus named supplementary hypotheses where required), not theorems of all GPTOPT models.

**Blocks:**

> `base GPTOPT -> reversible dilation / purification consequences` without the extra principle.

**Classification:** `NONFORCED` / conditionality witness.

## Cross-countermodel conclusion

Together these witnesses establish four separate selection burdens:

1. a quantum-selecting continuity/reversibility condition can matter;
2. local tomography does not uniquely fix global composition;
3. nonsignalling does not uniquely fix quantum correlations;
4. purification-derived structure cannot be credited to the base framework without purification.

They do **not** establish that any particular alternative is physically realized.

> **Countermodels defeat necessity claims; they do not automatically select a replacement theory.**
