# FCP-13 — CQM Reformulation / Anti-Laundering Countermodels

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Frameworks:** `FW-CQM`, quantum/QFT component of `FW-NULL-GRQFTSM`  
**Status:** NEGATIVE-WITNESS / SUBTRACTION-CONTROL CANDIDATE

## 0. Purpose

These witnesses block false promotion of generic categorical/process structure, model-level quantum representation or inherited empirical success into independent CQM foundational evidence.

## C1 — Generic monoidal-category witness

### Target overclaim

> CQM is compositional; therefore its compositional structure is uniquely quantum or independently foundational.

### Witness

Many non-quantum mathematical/process theories admit objects, morphisms, identities, sequential composition and symmetric monoidal/parallel composition.

### Result

Bare categorical/process composition fails the weaker-framework test.

`GENERIC PROCESS COMPOSITION != CQM-SPECIFIC PHYSICAL CONTENT`.

## C2 — Classical-circuit / stochastic-process witness

### Target overclaim

> Sequential and parallel composition of processes is distinctive evidence for CQM.

### Witness

Classical circuits, stochastic channels and other non-quantum process systems also support sequential and parallel composition with an interchange structure.

### Result

K3 receives CQM-specific credit only where explicit quantum-specialized structure survives generic subtraction.

## C3 — Diagrammatic-coherence witness

### Target overclaim

> String diagrams or diagrammatic isotopy provide independent physical evidence for quantum structure.

### Witness

String-diagrammatic calculi and coherence theorems occur in many categorical theories with unrelated physical interpretations.

### Result

Graphical convenience and generic coherence are presentational/mathematical until a quantum-specialized theorem and semantics are supplied.

`STRING-DIAGRAM ELEGANCE != EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE`.

## C4 — Toy/non-Hilbert model witness

### Target overclaim

> The minimal categorical/process axioms of CQM uniquely recover ordinary quantum mechanics.

### Witness

The frozen CQM intake explicitly recognizes relations/toy/non-Hilbert and other model-specific categorical realizations in addition to standard finite-dimensional Hilbert-space quantum theory.

### Result

Generic CQM is broader than ordinary quantum theory.

`TOY/NON-HILBERT MODELS BLOCK UNIQUE QUANTUM SELECTION FROM GENERIC PROCESS STRUCTURE`.

## C5 — Allowed-process vs. dynamics witness

### Target overclaim

> Because CQM contains quantum processes as morphisms, it supplies a physical dynamics/history selector.

### Witness

A category can contain many admissible processes while supplying no law, probability distribution or boundary/initial rule selecting which process occurs physically.

### Result

K3 process structure cannot be promoted to K4 dynamics.

`PROCESS COMPOSITION != HISTORY SELECTION`.

## C6 — Tensor/parallel vs. spacetime witness

### Target overclaim

> Monoidal parallel composition establishes physical spacetime locality or relativistic causality.

### Witness

Tensor/parallel composition occurs in theories with no Lorentzian spacetime, light cones, microcausality or relativistic propagation semantics.

### Result

Minimal CQM has no framework-level K6 causal bridge.

`MONOIDAL PARALLEL COMPOSITION != SPACETIME LOCALITY`.

## C7 — Model-calibration witness

### Target overclaim

> Empirical success of a standard CQM realization confirms abstract CQM as a framework.

### Witness

A concrete model such as standard finite-dimensional quantum theory can reproduce tested quantum predictions while the same abstract CQM umbrella also accommodates toy/nonphysical models.

### Result

Empirical success selects/constrains the concrete quantum realization, not every model in the containing categorical framework.

`FHILB SUCCESS != FRAMEWORK-WIDE CQM EMPIRICAL SELECTION`.

## C8 — Reformulation witness

### Target overclaim

> A quantum protocol derived diagrammatically and experimentally confirmed provides independent convergence evidence for CQM.

### Witness

The same quantum protocol can be represented in conventional Hilbert-space equations and in a categorical/string-diagram language with the same physical predictions because the categorical formulation was built to represent the quantum structure.

### Result

The structural relation may be strong, but its empirical support is inherited and its agreement is lineage-related.

`REFORMULATION != INDEPENDENT CONVERGENCE`.

## C9 — State-as-morphism ontology witness

### Target overclaim

> Representing a state as `I -> A` establishes a new physical ontology of states.

### Witness

The same syntax can represent states in many process theories with different underlying semantics.

### Result

State-as-morphism is a structural role, not an ontology selector.

## C10 — Compactness uniqueness witness

### Target overclaim

> Compact structure or cups/caps uniquely characterize quantum entanglement physics.

### Witness

Compact categories exist outside ordinary quantum theory and can possess map-state duality/cup-cap structure with non-quantum semantics.

### Result

Compactness is an optional structural ingredient; quantum physical meaning depends on the selected model.

## C11 — Frobenius/classical-structure uniqueness witness

### Target overclaim

> A Frobenius/classical structure automatically identifies the empirically correct quantum observable/classical interface.

### Witness

The categorical algebra requires a selected model and hypotheses; multiple such structures/models may exist and bare algebra does not supply calibration.

### Result

Classical/Frobenius structure is extension-dependent and model-relative, not framework-level empirical selection.

## C12 — Repeated-composition vs. RG witness

### Target overclaim

> Iterating/tensoring categorical processes supplies an RG/coarse-graining structure.

### Witness

Repeated composition can occur without any physical scale parameter, integration-out procedure, beta function, matching map or universality relation.

### Result

K7 remains open/no correspondence at FCP-13 scope.

## 1. Combined anti-laundering rule

A CQM result receives distinctive credit only after surviving all relevant witnesses:

1. generic category/process subtraction;
2. model-vs-framework separation;
3. optional-structure scoping;
4. physical-semantics test;
5. empirical-inheritance test;
6. independence test.

> **A MORE ECONOMICAL OR COMPOSITIONAL REPRESENTATION OF QUANTUM THEORY CAN BE SCIENTIFICALLY VALUABLE WITHOUT CONSTITUTING NEW PHYSICAL EVIDENCE.**
