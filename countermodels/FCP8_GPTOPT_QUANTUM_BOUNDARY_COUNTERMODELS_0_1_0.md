# FCP-8 — GPTOPT Quantum-Boundary Countermodels

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Framework:** `FW-GPTOPT`  
**Status:** COUNTERMODEL LEDGER CANDIDATE

## C1 — PR-box witness: no-signalling does not force quantum correlations

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP8-GPT-PR-1994`, `SRC-FCP8-GPT-TSIRELSON-1980`, `SRC-FCP4-GPT-BARRETT-2007`.

PR-box/superquantum correlations obey the declared nonsignalling condition while reaching CHSH strength outside the quantum set.

**Defeats:**

`no-signalling -> quantum correlation set`.

**Does not establish:** physical existence of PR boxes.

**Classification:** `COUNTERMODELED`.

---

## C2 — Composite ambiguity witness: local data do not force the quantum composite

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP4-GPT-MULLER-2021`, `SRC-FCP4-GPT-PLAVALA-2023`, FCP-7 baseline.

For fixed local GPT systems, multiple admissible composite constructions can exist between standard minimal and maximal bounds. Local tomography can fix representational recoverability of global states from local-product statistics without uniquely selecting the global positive cone/state space.

**Defeats:**

`local systems + local tomography -> unique quantum composite`.

**Classification:** `COUNTERMODELED` / `NONFORCED` depending on the exact claim.

---

## C3 — Principle insufficiency witness: almost-quantum correlations

**Source:** `SRC-FCP8-GPT-AQ-2015`.

The almost-quantum set strictly contains quantum correlations yet satisfies many proposed device-independent principles. The source reports numerical evidence concerning Information Causality; later/current IC work still reports gaps for some families.

**Defeats:**

`the currently bounded menu of broad information/macroscopic principles uniquely characterizes the quantum set`.

**Scope:** does not prove no future principle can uniquely characterize quantum correlations.

**Classification:** `COUNTERMODELED` at current source scope.

---

## C4 — Tomography incompleteness witness

**Source:** `SRC-FCP7-GPT-MAZUREK-2021`.

A finite operational GPT tomography experiment constrains the state/effect geometry only if the implemented preparation and measurement families are tomographically complete for the system under study. Hidden/unprobed degrees of freedom remain the source's principal loophole.

**Defeats:**

`finite tested probability table -> exhaustive physical GPT state/effect space`.

**Classification:** `NONFORCED`.

---

## C5 — Apparent Tsirelson violation from postselection does not establish post-quantum physics

**Source:** `SRC-FCP8-GPT-RINGBAUER-2014`.

The experiment generates apparent supraquantum correlations using polarization-dependent loss and postselection within ordinary quantum optics, reaching apparent values beyond Tsirelson's bound. This demonstrates how sampling/postselection can emulate the relevant statistics.

**Defeats:**

`observed postselected CHSH value above Tsirelson -> natural post-quantum law observed`.

**Classification:** `COUNTERMODELED` as an empirical-inference warning.

---

## C6 — Information Causality partial-bound witness

**Sources:** `SRC-FCP8-GPT-IC-2009`, `SRC-FCP8-GPT-IC-2026`.

Information Causality can recover important quantum boundaries but the current 2026 source explicitly retains gaps for some correlation families.

**Defeats:**

`Information Causality as currently source-bound -> complete quantum correlation set`.

**Classification:** `NONFORCED` / `OPEN`.

---

# Countermodel conclusion

The FCP-8 negative witnesses jointly establish a disciplined ceiling:

> **No-signalling, local tomography, finite operational tomography, one candidate information principle, or one observed Bell/correlation slice is sufficient by itself to select the complete quantum theory from the broader GPTOPT possibility space.**

They do not establish that quantum theory lacks a deeper principled explanation; they establish only that the bounded candidate explanations are presently nonunique/incomplete.
