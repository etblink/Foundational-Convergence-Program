**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** COUNTERMODEL CANDIDATE  
**Frameworks:** `FW-NFC-RED`, `FW-AS`

## 0. Purpose

These negative witnesses test whether tempting NFC/AS correspondences follow from the exact closed packet. They do not refute either framework. They delimit what can be inferred under the dual firewall.

## CM-01 — RG fixed point without finite partition stabilization

Take a continuous/infinite-dimensional theory-space flow with an interacting fixed point. No finite carrier or monotone partition chain is required.

Defeats:

`RG_FIXED_POINT -> NFC_FIXED_CARRIER_STABILIZATION`.

Preserves AS-R1 while showing NFC-R4 is not structurally forced.

## CM-02 — finite partition stabilization without RG fixed point

Take any monotone partition refinement on a fixed finite set. It eventually stabilizes by finiteness even when no RG scale, beta function, running coupling or physical fixed point exists.

Defeats:

`NFC_FIXED_CARRIER_STABILIZATION -> AS_RG_FIXED_POINT`.
