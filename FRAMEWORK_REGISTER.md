## Admission rule

Admission means only that a framework is sufficiently relevant to preserve a comparison slot. Framework families may later need subdivision before source-bound analysis; subdivision must happen before scientific scoring, not after seeing a favorable result.

## No inherited credit

Reduced NFC receives no special status from having motivated the FCP. Likewise, established physics receives empirical credit only for evidence actually supporting it and no automatic credit for deeper ontological claims it does not make.
