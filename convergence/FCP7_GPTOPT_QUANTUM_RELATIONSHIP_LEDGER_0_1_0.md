## 3. Generalization credit

FCP-7 identifies four K-keys with clear broader-than-quantum theory-space content:

- K1 — state-space class;
- K3 — transformation/process class;
- K5 — effect/measurement class;
- K8 — composite-system possibilities.

This is framework-specific scientific structure in the sense that GPTOPT explicitly parameterizes physically interpretable alternatives to the quantum choices. It is **not** evidence that any post-quantum member is realized by nature.

## 4. Additional-axiom credit

K6 is the clearest primary `A` key because physically important restrictions such as nonsignalling, operational causality and local tomography are not interchangeable generic consequences of the base framework.

## 5. Structural sharpening credit

K2 and K9 are useful even without selecting a new physical theory:

- K2 forces a clean distinction between preparation procedure and operational state/equivalence class;
- K9 separates laboratory-facing operational semantics from full physical calibration.

These are conceptual/structural clarifications, not independent empirical confirmation of GPTOPT.

## 6. Empirical constraint credit

The source does not prove that all physically possible GPTs reduce to QM, does not fix global composite rules, and does not empirically establish any reconstruction axiom package.
