# FCP-3 — NFC / Null Divergence Ledger

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Frameworks:** `FW-NFC-RED`, `FW-NULL-GRQFTSM`

A divergence is not automatically a defect. Each entry records whether the difference is a scope difference, missing structure, unresolved correspondence, or materially different physical semantics.

## D1 — K1 Carrier architecture

**Reduced NFC:** finite relational/operational arenas and test-relative quotient descriptions.  
**Null:** continuum spacetime/field configurations and quantum state structures in established domains.  
**Type:** `DIFFERENCE_IN_SCOPE + UNRESOLVED_CORRESPONDENCE`.  
**Why material:** no source-bound map shows that the NFC carrier realizes or recovers the null physical state carriers.  
**Not implied:** the continuum carrier is uniquely fundamental; the null baseline itself has no unified quantum-gravity carrier.

## D2 — K2 Equivalence semantics

**Reduced NFC:** observational equivalence relative to selected tests means indistinguishability under `T`.  
**Null:** gauge/diffeomorphism redundancy identifies physically redundant representatives at the component-theory level.  
**Type:** `MATERIALLY_DIFFERENT_PHYSICAL_SEMANTICS`.  
**Why material:** epistemic/test-relative indistinguishability is not the same claim as physical sameness.  
**Shared residue:** generic quotient mathematics only.

## D3 — K4 Dynamics / occurrence

**Reduced NFC:** maximal admissible processes define possibilities; no global source-derived physical-history selector survives the reduction.  
**Null:** GR and SM/QFT contain genuine sector dynamics conditional on required data.  
**Type:** `MISSING_STRUCTURE_ON_NFC_SIDE`.  
**Why material:** K4 distinguishes possible transformations from laws selecting/weighting physical histories.  
**Null limitation preserved:** no single established UV-complete quantum-gravity history law or unique cosmic initial condition.

## D4 — K5 Measurement semantics

**Reduced NFC:** formal selected test algebra/family and observational quotient; general physical exhaustion/calibration unproved.  
**Null:** operational observables tied to clocks, interferometers, scattering, decay, spectra, event rates, etc.  
**Type:** `MISSING_PHYSICAL_BRIDGE_ON_NFC_SIDE`.  
**Why material:** a formal observable family and a detector/calibration interface occupy different evidentiary levels.

## D5 — K6 Locality / causality

**Reduced NFC:** combinatorial collars, interfaces, bounded/local detectability, conditional screening.  
**Null:** Lorentzian light-cone causality, relativistic propagation, QFT microcausality/local interaction structure.  
**Type:** `MATERIALLY_DIFFERENT_PHYSICAL_SEMANTICS + UNRESOLVED_CORRESPONDENCE`.  
**Why material:** adjacency/interface structure has no source-bound signalling or causal realization map.

## D6 — K7 Scale architecture

**Reduced NFC:** refinement, finite stabilization, and declared transport among descriptions/regimes.  
**Null:** renormalization-group evolution, running couplings, EFT matching, controlled approximations tied to physical scales.  
**Type:** `UNRESOLVED_CORRESPONDENCE`.  
**Why material:** no common scale parameter, flow law, invariant observable set, or controlled limit is established.

## D7 — K8 Globalization maturity

**Reduced NFC:** globalization remains principally an open question; universal completion is relative to a supplied category/diagram.  
**Null:** specific theories possess concrete patching, constraint, bundle, anomaly, and global-consistency machinery.  
**Type:** `DIFFERENCE_IN_MATURITY/SCOPE`.  
**Why material:** generic recognition of a local-to-global burden is weaker than a source-derived physical consistency mechanism.  
**Shared residue:** weak/generic local-to-global logic only.

## D8 — K9 Physical realization / calibration

**Reduced NFC:** no general reduced-foundational map to calibrated time, length, energy, mass, charge, curvature, species, detector records, cross sections, or frequencies. Historical branch realizations are declared/conditional and cannot be promoted.  
**Null:** mature calibration/realization chains in tested domains.  
**Type:** `MISSING_STRUCTURE_ON_NFC_SIDE`.  
**Why material:** this is the bridge from mathematics to physical theory.

## D9 — K10 Empirical status

**Reduced NFC:** no current foundational prediction satisfying FCP K10 against the null comparator.  
**Null:** many sector-level quantitative tests with empirical support, while foundational uniqueness remains unclaimed.  
**Type:** `MISSING_CURRENT_DISCRIMINATOR_ON_NFC_SIDE`.  
**Why material:** absent discrimination prevents present empirical selection of Reduced NFC over the null baseline.

# Non-divergence control — K3 allowed transformations

FCP-3 does **not** create a separate material divergence solely because the transformation categories differ. Both frameworks have domain-relative typed transformation structures, but no E1–E4 map identifies them. The present status is weak/generic recurrence plus unresolved physical correspondence, not a contradiction.

# Open-problem firewall

The following null-baseline frontiers do not reduce any divergence above and confer no credit on Reduced NFC:

- UV-complete quantum gravity;
- parameter origin;
- neutrino-mass mechanism;
- dark-matter identity;
- dark-energy explanation;
- quantum interpretation;
- cosmological initial conditions.

Conversely, Reduced-NFC gaps do not confer extra credit on the null baseline beyond what the null sources actually establish.
