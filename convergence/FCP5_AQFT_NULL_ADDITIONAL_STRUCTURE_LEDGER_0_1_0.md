# FCP-5 — AQFT / Null Additional-Structure Ledger

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Purpose:** isolate source-qualified AQFT structure that is not exhausted by the bounded null QFT presentation while preventing generic or assumed structure from being overstated.

## Classification guard

An entry here is not automatically evidence of a distinct physical theory. It records additional structure relative to the bounded null presentation.

Each entry must distinguish:

- supplied axiom/organization;
- derived theorem/consequence;
- physical interpretation;
- empirical distinction.

## Ledger

### `AS-01` — LCQFT functorial covariance across spacetimes

- **Keys:** K3, K8
- **Sources:** `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-BFV-2003`, `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-FV-2015`
- **AQFT structure:** a QFT is organized as a covariant functor from globally hyperbolic spacetimes with admissible embeddings to algebras with injective structure-preserving morphisms.
- **Null relation:** the bounded null baseline contains QFT on spacetime but did not source-bind one universal functorial cross-background doctrine.
- **Descriptor:** `MODEL_CLASS_EXTENSION`.
- **E-class:** not independent convergence; fixed-background Haag–Kastler theories are recovered as special cases in the LCQFT source lineage.
- **Genericization:** category/functor mathematics is generic; the physical specialization to locally covariant QFT is not merely the generic shell.
- **Selection status:** framework assumption/organization plus derived consequences; globally hyperbolic Lorentzian geometry remains supplied.
- **Scope ceiling:** structural/model-class extension of QFT across classical backgrounds; not quantum gravity and not derivation of causal geometry.

### `AS-02` — Relative Cauchy evolution

- **Keys:** K4, K8
- **Sources:** `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-BFV-2003`, `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-FV-2015`
- **AQFT structure:** for locally covariant theories satisfying appropriate time-slice conditions, automorphisms encode response to local changes of background metric; functional metric variation is related to stress-energy structure in the cited development.
- **Descriptor:** `STRUCTURAL_REFINEMENT` with extension-level dynamical-response content.
- **Not:** a universal AQFT history selector.
- **Genericization:** automorphisms are generic; the metric-response construction and its QFT interpretation require the LCQFT/time-slice hypotheses.
- **Scope ceiling:** conditional response/dynamical sensitivity to supplied background perturbations.

### `AS-03` — No preferred locally covariant state in general

- **Keys:** K1, K8
- **Source:** `SRC-FCP4-AQFT-FV-2015`
- **AQFT structure:** the modern LCQFT synthesis records an obstruction to assigning a single preferred state in every spacetime while maintaining the stated locality/covariance requirements.
- **Descriptor:** `STRUCTURAL_REFINEMENT` / negative selection result.
- **Genericization:** not merely the existence of multiple states; the source-qualified no-natural-state result depends on the LCQFT setting and assumptions.
- **Scope ceiling:** constrains natural state-selection prescriptions; does not prove that no physically motivated state-selection rule can exist under different/nonlocal/additional assumptions.

### `AS-05` — pAQFT interacting/renormalized model construction

- **Key:** K7, with K4/K9 relevance

- **Scope ceiling:** extension/model construction; not an intrinsic RG law of minimal AQFT and not an independent empirical selection of AQFT.

## Rejected novelty promotions

The following promotions are not supported:

1. **AQFT derives relativistic causality** — rejected. Causal/geometric structure is supplied and locality is axiomatically imposed in the relevant formulations.
2. **LCQFT derives spacetime** — rejected. Globally hyperbolic Lorentzian spacetimes are source-category objects.
3. **Relative Cauchy evolution is universal physical dynamics** — rejected. It is a conditional response structure under additional assumptions.
4. **AQFT predicts Standard Model parameters** — rejected. Concrete numerical content is model/experiment supplied.
5. **pAQFT renormalization proves AQFT-specific empirical superiority** — rejected. No framework-level discriminator is source-bound.
