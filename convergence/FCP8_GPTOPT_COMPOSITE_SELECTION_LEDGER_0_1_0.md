## 3. Local tomography result

At the FCP-4/FCP-7 source scope, local tomography provides a rule by which suitable local-product measurement statistics determine the global state representation. It does **not** uniquely determine which positive cone/global state set inside the tensor-product vector space is physically realized.

Accordingly:

> **LOCAL TOMOGRAPHY != UNIQUE QUANTUM COMPOSITE.**

The minimal/maximal bounds and intermediate composite choices remain a `MODEL_CHOICE`/`OPEN` burden unless additional source-qualified principles or empirical data select one.

## 4. Bell data versus full composite selection

Bell experiments observe a correlation projection:

`composite state/effect structure -> p(a,b|x,y)`.

Different deeper state/effect/composite structures can in principle project to overlapping finite correlation data. Therefore:

> **EXCLUDING A CORRELATION REGION DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY EXCLUDE EVERY GPT COMPOSITE THAT COULD PRODUCE THE REMAINING REGION.**

FCP-8 identifies no theorem lifting existing Bell/GPT empirical bounds to unique selection of the entire quantum composite state space.

## 5. Maximal-composite empirical status

The full maximal composite envelope is not empirically realized merely because it is mathematically admissible. Likewise, present correlation experiments do not source-bind a complete quantitative volume/fraction of the maximal composite cone that is excluded in all dimensions and all measurement settings.

Status:

`OPEN-SELECTION`.

Question 11 of FCP-8 therefore receives:

> **No source-qualified global fraction of the maximal composite theory space is established as experimentally excluded. Only bounded operational/correlation slices are constrained.**

## 7. Composite selection conclusion

> **The strongest FCP-8 K8 result is underdetermination: local operational structure plus commonly studied consistency/tomographic principles does not source-select one unique global quantum composite, and current empirical evidence constrains projections/low-dimensional realizations rather than the complete GPT composite theory space.**
