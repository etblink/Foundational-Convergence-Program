## 7. Upgrade conditions

A future result may supersede this ledger only if it supplies at least one of:

- explicit E1 structural equivalence;
- explicit E2 functor/representation preserving material operations and observables;
- E3 controlled physical limit with error bounds;
- E4 empirical equivalence/discrimination with declared tolerance;
- a non-generic quantitative structure independently derived on both sides.

Merely finding more examples of quotienting, locality, categories, or scale dependence cannot upgrade the current result.
