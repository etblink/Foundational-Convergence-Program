> **Mathematical success is not independent empirical selection.**

## 2. Almost-quantum counterpressure

`SRC-FCP8-GPT-AQ-2015` defines an `almost quantum` set that strictly contains the quantum set while satisfying many proposed device-independent quantum-correlation principles. The paper proves satisfaction of several named principles and reports numerical evidence regarding Information Causality.

FCP-8 uses this set as a **principle-insufficiency countermodel**, not as proof that one physically realizable post-quantum theory exists.

The current 2026 IC source independently strengthens the conservative conclusion: improved Information Causality analysis closes some gaps, but the source still reports other correlation families with a gap between the IC boundary and the quantum set.

Therefore:

## 3. No principle stacking

FCP-8 does not form a synthetic package `No-signalling + IC + ML + ...` and then call the intersection fundamental.

A combined package would require an independent selector explaining:

1. why those principles;
2. why no alternatives;
3. why their exact mathematical strength;
4. whether each is physically realized;
5. whether the combination is necessary rather than merely sufficient for the desired target.

No such selector is source-bound here.

## 4. Information Causality laboratory control

`SRC-FCP8-GPT-RINGBAUER-2014` implements the IC protocol experimentally and demonstrates violation for **emulated** supraquantum correlations generated using loss and postselection.

Classification:

- evidence hierarchy: `E3` principle-test methodology;
- principle independently selected by nature: **NO**;
- natural postquantum correlations observed: **NO**;
- useful result: the operational IC task is experimentally implementable and distinguishes the emulated statistics in the tested regime.

The experiment cannot be used to infer that post-quantum physics exists, nor that IC uniquely explains nature's quantum boundary.
