# FCP-12 — Reduced NFC / CST Survivor-Test Ledger

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** SURVIVOR-QUESTION TEST CANDIDATE

## 0. Purpose

This ledger tests the six Reduced-NFC questions that survived prior reduction against only the FCP-11-qualified CST residue. A `PASS` here would require a non-generic source-bound CST counterpart, not merely that the question can be asked of CST.

Statuses:

- `PASS_NON_GENERIC` — source-bound non-generic correspondence survives;
- `UNRESOLVED_DISCOVERY` — scientifically meaningful question, but no required source-bound theorem/map is present;
- `DEFEATED_AS_CONVERGENCE` — the apparent correspondence is generic or a material asymmetry defeats it at current scope.

## 1. Congruence

Reduced-NFC form:

`pi ∘ E = E_bar ∘ pi`.

Required CST instantiation:

- selected fundamental CST dynamics `E`;
- declared projection/coarse-graining `pi`;
- effective continuum evolution `E_bar`;
- exact factorization;
- preserved physical structure;
- no laundering through a supplied manifold target.

Frozen-source result:

No such theorem is identified. Selected CST operator/action continuum limits do not establish factorization of actual selected histories.

Verdict:

`NO_SOURCE_BOUND_CST_CONGRUENCE_ANALOGUE_AT_CURRENT_SCOPE`

Status: `UNRESOLVED_DISCOVERY`.

## 2. Viability

Reduced-NFC form:

persistence/invariance of an admissible set under declared dynamics.

Potential CST target:

manifoldlike causal sets under a selected CSG/QSG/action-based dynamics.

Required distinction:

1. define manifoldlike sector;
2. prove invariance or attraction under selected dynamics;
3. prove preferential physical selection;
4. recover GR-like behavior;
5. empirical selection.

The frozen CST packet does not establish step 2 framework-wide, and therefore cannot support the stronger steps.

Verdict:

`NO_SOURCE_BOUND_CST_MANIFOLD_VIABILITY_THEOREM_AT_CURRENT_SCOPE`

Status: `UNRESOLVED_DISCOVERY`.

This is a scientifically real CST dynamics/manifold-selection question but not present convergence.

## 3. Interface Sufficiency

Reduced-NFC form:

`q = Phi ∘ c`, equivalently `ker c ⊆ ker q`.

Required CST instantiation:

- finite causal boundary/interface statistic `c`;
- declared exterior/global physical outcome `q`;
- source-bound factorization through `c`;
- exact scope and physical semantics.

The frozen CST packet's stems, intervals, local order data and continuum estimators do not establish this factorization.

Verdict:

`NO_SOURCE_BOUND_CST_FIS_ANALOGUE_AT_CURRENT_SCOPE`

Status: `UNRESOLVED_DISCOVERY`.

BLIN/R6/R7 receive no CST-specific upgrade because the necessary FIS/interface-capacity premises are not source-bound in CST.

## 5. Realization

Reduced NFC explicitly lacks a general calibrated physical bridge.

CST has bounded physical semantics:

- order ↔ causal structure;
- number/cardinality ↔ continuum volume information;
- selected reconstruction estimators/operators.

No independently source-bound map translates Reduced-NFC formal structures into that CST physical bridge. Post-hoc interpretation is prohibited.

Verdict:

`MATERIAL_REALIZATION_ASYMMETRY`

Status: `DEFEATED_AS_CONVERGENCE`.

Verdict:

`MATERIAL_DYNAMICS_ASYMMETRY`

Status: `DEFEATED_AS_CONVERGENCE`.
