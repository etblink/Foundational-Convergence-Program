# FCP-5 — AQFT / Null Reformulation Ledger

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Frameworks:** `FW-AQFT`, `FW-NULL-GRQFTSM`  
**Purpose:** prevent expected QFT–AQFT agreement from being miscounted as independent convergence.

## Rule

AQFT arose as an algebraic formulation and later extension of QFT. Therefore structural agreement receives the label `REFORMULATION_RELATION` unless a genuinely independent derivational relation is established.

`REFORMULATION_RELATION` is not a negative label. It means agreement is historically/mathematically dependent and therefore cannot be used as independent convergence evidence.

## Generic-only exclusions

The following do not earn AQFT-specific convergence credit by themselves:

1. operator-algebra representation/isomorphism machinery;
2. categorical/functorial composition;
3. abstract nets/isotony/gluing;
4. generic perturbative/renormalization techniques.

## Consequence for later FCP comparisons

A future NFC–AQFT resemblance to an entry in this ledger cannot be promoted merely because AQFT and successful QFT share it. The later comparison must first subtract the reformulational/generic content recorded here and test only the non-generic AQFT residue.
