## 1. Search and intake protocol

The canonical FCP source registers and framework packets were searched first to reconstruct the prior bound objects. The new search then followed theorem/result papers to their journal or arXiv records, checked current arXiv versions and withdrawal status, and sought supporting, limiting, critical, counterexample, and current-review evidence for each track. Search-result snippets were discovery aids only; admissions bind the publication or arXiv record named below. A source was admitted only when it materially bore on the declared gap and its assumptions and scope could be stated.

```text
SEARCHED_FOR_POSITIVE_EVIDENCE_AQFT = YES
SEARCHED_FOR_NEGATIVE_OR_LIMITING_EVIDENCE_AQFT = YES
```

All arXiv versions below are the versions inspected on 2026-08-25. Published records are not silently replaced by a later preprint version. Rejected candidates remain visible so that non-admission is not confused with non-discovery.
