### Body-level evidence extracted from the exact published paper

The paper's lane-material content is more specific than the admitted review's summary:

1. **Ten-dimensional context.** The analysis concerns chiral ten-dimensional supersymmetric Yang-Mills coupled to `N=1, D=10` supergravity and the consistency requirement that gauge and gravitational anomalies vanish.
2. **Higher-derivative/local interactions.** The minimal low-energy action is supplemented by higher-derivative local interactions from the superstring low-energy expansion; their non-invariant variations cancel fermion-loop anomalies.
3. **B-field/Chern–Simons mechanism.** The two-form field strength is modified by Yang–Mills and Lorentz Chern–Simons terms and the B field transforms so that the modified field strength is invariant.
4. **Gauge anomaly condition.** The uncancellable `tr F^6` structure disappears for `SO(32)`; the remaining factorized terms can be cancelled by local interactions.
5. **Gravitational anomaly condition.** The irreducible gravitational contribution requires 496 chiral matter fermions, matching the adjoint dimension of both `SO(32)` and `E8 × E8`.
6. **Mixed anomalies.** The local terms used for pure gauge and gravitational anomalies also cancel the mixed anomalies; for `SO(32)` the paper states complete cancellation of all anomalies at one-loop level.
7. **E8 x E8.** The effective `E8 × E8` theory likewise has the required one-loop cancellation, but the paper expressly states that the corresponding superstring theory had not yet been formulated.
8. **String result and limitations.** The `SO(32)` type-I result is connected to explicit one-loop string hexagon calculations. The conclusion still leaves further closed-string anomaly calculations and possible anomalies under large gauge transformations to future work.

These details are primary provenance and are not fully reproduced by the Agmon review. They are nevertheless consistent with, rather than corrective of, the bounded FCP-24 foundational/perturbative-consistency picture.
