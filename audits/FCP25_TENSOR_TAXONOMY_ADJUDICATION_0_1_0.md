This is a bounded classification, not a claim that tensor networks, quantum information, holography, or entanglement-based reconstruction lack scientific importance. It says that the frozen evidence does not bind their historical conjunction into a stable foundational competitor and does not source-bind any scientifically adequate successor framework.

The determining facts are:

1. MPS, PEPS, DMRG, tensor-network renormalization, TDVP, MERA, and cMERA are powerful mathematical, representational, variational, computational, or RG architectures. Their frozen sources take ordinary quantum states, partition functions, quantum fields, lattice models, reference states, or Hamiltonians as supplied inputs. Tensor-network coordinates and algorithms do not thereby become a new physical ontology or history selector.
2. Holographic tensor networks and QEC constructions form precise model and reconstruction families. In the frozen corpus their physical interpretation is principally conditioned on AdS/CFT, CFT code subspaces, semiclassical bulk targets, RT-like entropy structure, a chosen graph/code, or related supplied holographic inputs. The network or code does not independently select microscopic spacetime degrees of freedom or bulk dynamics.
3. The information-first sources are not merely formulations of one object. They use materially different carriers and premises: boundary-QFT factorization and AdS/CFT entanglement, small-ball QFT vacuum entanglement on classical spacetime, or a chosen Hilbert-space factorization with a mutual-information graph and embedding procedure. The corpus supplies no shared primitive carrier, model class, equivalence rule, internal dynamics, or realization bridge binding them into one framework.
4. The admitted limitation sources block promotion by area law, representability, conventional AdS/MERA identification, or a unique geometric reading. The experimental sources establish prepared-state or code realizations, not empirical selection of a fundamental spacetime architecture.
