## 4. Track A — AQFT

### 4.1 Scientific disposition

For a suitable inclusion of nested local regions with positive separation, the split property interposes a type-I factor. In the standard setting this gives a spatial tensor-product representation and normal product-state extension for the separated subsystem algebras. Energy and modular nuclearity conditions control localized phase-space density and imply split only under their respective hypotheses. Some modular formulations provide converse-like characterizations at specified inclusion scope; there is no unrestricted converse from split to every nuclearity condition.

The structure occurs across multiple free, conformal, locally covariant and interacting integrable model classes. It is not a minimal AQFT axiom. Möbius covariance alone is insufficient; conformal counterexamples exist; species/mass proliferation can defeat the relevant bounds or leave only distal split; and the type-I interposition is collar-dependent and generally infinite-dimensional.
