### A — primitive/object class

The frozen corpus source-binds local quantum laboratories/operations and a global process-matrix object whose role is to assign joint probabilities to local interventions without presupposing one fixed global causal order. This is a stable physical/operational object class, not generic category theory or notation.

### B — coherent process/model domain

The corpus establishes a coherent domain of valid multipartite process matrices, causal/causally separable and causally nonseparable subclasses, higher-order transformations, and explicit validity/composition questions. The domain is broader than the quantum switch and narrower than arbitrary operational theory.

### C — physical law/constraint architecture

`C = PASS` under Method 0.2.1 because process-matrix positivity/normalization/consistency conditions are physically interpreted constraints ensuring valid probabilities for arbitrary allowed local interventions. Causal/separability definitions and composition-validity restrictions add source-bound physical process constraints.

This is not a claim that the framework selects one actual history. It does not. Dynamics/transformations are optional and representation-sensitive. The pass is for physical constraint architecture, consistently with the prospective Method 0.2.1 rule.

### D — stable physical scope/model class

The stable core scope is generalized operational physics of local quantum laboratories linked by global processes without a presupposed fixed global causal order. The corpus repeatedly preserves this scope across definitions, causal classifications, composition, transformations, realizability, resources and experiments.

### E — physical interpretation/realization burden

Local laboratories, interventions, probabilities, causal witnesses, realizability constructions and explicit spacetime/closed-lab restrictions provide a nontrivial physical-interpretation and realization burden. The framework does not obtain a free pass from formal validity to physical realizability.

### F — cross-source continuity

Continuity from the 2012 process-matrix core through causal classification, composition, transformations, realizability, resource structure, current synthesis and experimental boundary work is sufficient to identify an object-specific later K1–K10 baseline without importing a different family.

### G — principled distinction

The framework is not identity-covered by `FW-CQM` or `FW-GPTOPT`.

`FW-CQM` supplies quantum process/compositional semantics but explicitly has no intrinsic causal structure and no generic higher-order global process-matrix primitive. `FW-GPTOPT` supplies a general operational theory space and allowed transformations but does not source-bind the process-matrix global causal-process object or its no-fixed-global-order consistency architecture as framework identity.

Containment/representation overlap therefore does not collapse identity, just as the existing FCP taxonomy keeps `FW-CQM` distinct from the broader `FW-GPTOPT` meta-framework despite quantum theory being an admissible GPTOPT model.

### H — empirical/observational burden

The framework bears explicit empirical burdens through local-intervention probability predictions, causal correlations, causal inequalities/witnesses, realizability restrictions, and incompatibility between formally valid and physically implementable sectors. No framework-level empirical selection is claimed.
