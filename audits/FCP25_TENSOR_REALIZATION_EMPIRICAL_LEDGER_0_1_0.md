## 8. Open realization and empirical burdens

The frozen corpus leaves the following burdens open for any future, separately sourced foundational proposal:

- selection of the physical carrier, tensorization, factorization, graph, code or network geometry;
- distinction between representational gauge and physical equivalence;
- an internally selected law of physical histories rather than supplied-Hamiltonian evolution, optimization or RG flow;
- a source-qualified bridge from graph/network/code relations to Lorentzian influence and calibrated spacetime geometry;
- realistic and controlled continuum, Lorentzian and time-dependent dynamics;
- a physical code or QEC mechanism rather than only a structural description;
- operational observables, units, calibration and uncertainty propagation tied to the foundational claim;
- a comparator and risky, decision-qualified empirical prediction that is not inherited from a represented or target theory.

These are `OPEN_PROBLEM` or `NO_GENERAL_RESULT` classifications, not proofs of impossibility.
