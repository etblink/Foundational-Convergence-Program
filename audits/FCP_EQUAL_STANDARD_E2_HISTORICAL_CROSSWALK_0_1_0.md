## 2. Application inconsistency

`HISTORICAL_E2_APPLICATION_INCONSISTENCY = CONFIRMED`

The inconsistency is not that FCP-14 necessarily deserved E2. It is that FCP-14 treated failure to reproduce the exact bridge construction inside the internal packet as sufficient to prevent source qualification even though the source was already admitted, whereas FCP-5 and FCP-13 permitted bounded source-derived representation relations at concrete/model scope.

This created a provenance implementation asymmetry:

`ALREADY_BOUND_SOURCE_SUPPORT` was usable in FCP-5/FCP-13, while FCP-14 effectively required `ALREADY_BOUND_SOURCE_SUPPORT + INTERNAL_PACKET_TRANSCRIPTION`.

The latter requirement is not an additional scientific predicate in frozen FCP-2.
