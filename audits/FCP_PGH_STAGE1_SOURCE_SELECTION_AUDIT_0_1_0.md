## 4. Admission reasoning by source family

### 4.1 Fritz 2012 / 2014 — mandatory prior-art controls

**Admitted.** These are foundational primary sources for correlation scenarios and arbitrary causal structures with latent variables. They directly bear on whether `PGH-GRAM-0010` is novel framework mathematics or an instantiation of established causal/network-local machinery.

Without these sources, Stage 1 would risk granting PGH novelty for formal structure that substantially predates it.

### 4.2 Henson-Lal-Pusey 2014 — mandatory nontriviality control

**Admitted.** This source independently establishes that generalized Bayesian-network structure can impose theory-independent observable restrictions beyond ordinary conditional independence, including a three-party perfect-correlation exclusion with only bipartite common causes.

It is therefore load-bearing counterpressure against treating PGH's common-bit exclusion as uniquely PGH physical evidence.

### 4.3 Inflation sources — mandatory generic membership-method controls

**Admitted.** Wolfe-Spekkens-Fritz 2016 and Navascués-Wolfe 2017 establish that latent-variable causal compatibility, including triangle-like structures, has a mature generic algorithmic/mathematical theory. They are used to classify membership testing as prior art, not to test PGH empirically.

### 4.4 Quantum triangle sources — mandatory physical counterboundary

**Admitted.** Fraser-Wolfe 2017, Renou et al. 2019, Pozas-Kerstjens et al. 2023, and Don et al. 2026 establish increasingly sharp separations between the classical independent-source triangle class and quantum/post-quantum network possibilities.

The 2026 Don et al. result is especially important because it reaches the no-input, binary-output triangle, closely matching the formal alphabet of PGH's native-binary interface.

These sources are **not** used as a PGH empirical test. They establish only that the classical triangle class is not externally known as a universal physical possibility class and that PGH's physical postulate is nontrivial relative to quantum-network theory.

### 4.5 Suprano et al. 2022 — physical-realization boundary

**Admitted.** This experimental source prevents the intake from treating quantum triangle distinctions as merely formal. Its role is implementation-level physical boundary only; its exact measurement protocol is not silently identified with PGH's target protocol.
