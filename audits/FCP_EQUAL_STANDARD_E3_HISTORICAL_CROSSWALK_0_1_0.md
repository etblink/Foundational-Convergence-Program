## 0. Frozen E3 proposition

Under `FCP_EQUIVALENCE_AND_CONVERGENCE_RULES_0_1_0`, E3 is a controlled-limit/recovery relation. The required record names a control parameter/regime, target quantities, convergence/error notion, validity domain, surviving/failing structure, and whether physical calibration is preserved.

The frozen rule requires calibration status to be recorded. It does not state that detector-level calibration must be preserved before any mathematical substructure can receive bounded E3.

## 2. Equal-burden reconstruction

The CST precedent demonstrates the historical operational burden actually accepted by FCP:

1. identify a genuine source-defined asymptotic/continuum/controlled regime;
2. identify the source and target quantities/structures;
3. retain the declared hypotheses and validity scope;
4. record the limited recovery as E3;
5. separately refuse promotion to full dynamics/emergence/physical calibration/independent convergence.

The LOOP and AS comparisons instead converted items (4) and (5) into a stricter all-or-nothing gate.

`HISTORICAL_E3_APPLICATION_INCONSISTENCY = CONFIRMED`
