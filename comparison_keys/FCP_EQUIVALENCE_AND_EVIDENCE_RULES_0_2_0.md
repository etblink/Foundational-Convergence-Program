# FCP Equivalence, Recovery and Evidence Rules — Prospective 0.2.0

**Version:** 0.2.0  
**Status:** PROSPECTIVE GOVERNANCE CANDIDATE  
**Supersession:** if later integrated, governs future comparisons only unless a separate historical reanalysis is authorized. Historical `FCP_EQUIVALENCE_AND_CONVERGENCE_RULES_0_1_0` remains immutable.

## 0. Purpose

Method 0.2.0 retains the strict map/limit disciplines of 0.1.0 while removing the inference that a relation class, independence status, viability status and empirical selection are one dimension.

## 1. E1 — exact structural relation

Requirements:
- explicit source objects;
- map/equivalence;
- domain/codomain;
- preserved material structure;
- inverse/equivalence data as applicable;
- scope.

No framework-wide promotion from a reduced object.

## 2. E2 — representation relation

Requirements:
- typed translation/map/functor/representation;
- preserved and nonpreserved structure;
- faithfulness/fullness/invertibility where relevant;
- physical/observable scope;
- scope ceiling.

Provenance:
- original already-bound sources may be inspected directly;
- transcription into the current packet is optional documentation, not an extra scientific theorem;
- a mere statement of “bridge” or common encompassing formalism is insufficient.

## 3. E3 — controlled recovery relation

Requirements:
- control parameter/regime;
- source quantity/structure;
- target quantity/structure;
- convergence/asymptotic/error relation;
- hypotheses/validity domain;
- surviving/failing structure;
- calibration status;
- scope ceiling.

Subtypes:
- `E3-S` substructure;
- `E3-M` model/extension;
- `E3-F` framework-level;
- `E3-P` full physical realization.

A mathematical E3-S/E3-M can exist with `CAL_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Lack of calibration blocks unsupported physical/empirical promotion rather than erasing the mathematical controlled relation.

## 4. E4 — operational predictive relation

Requirements:
- declared observable set;
- preparation/initial conditions;
- parameter treatment;
- uncertainty/tolerance model;
- test domain;
- known out-of-scope observables.

E4 states a relation among predictions. It does not itself mean that either framework has been independently selected by data.

Empirical consequences are recorded separately:
`EMP0`, `EMP1`, `EMP2`, `EMP3`, `EMP4`, `EMP-NEG`, `EMP-U`.

## 5. E5 — functional relation

Use only when a source-qualified functional/organizational role relation exists but E1–E4 do not.

Do not use E5 as an automatic fallback for:
- generic mathematics;
- lineage;
- target conditioning;
- packet incompleteness;
- unknown relation;
- absence of evidence.

Use `NONE_ESTABLISHED` or `UNRESOLVED_UNDER_FROZEN_CORPUS` instead where appropriate.

## 6. Independence

Relation and independence are independent fields.

Strong evidence of independently recurring structure prospectively requires:
- E1–E4 relation at relevant scope;
- `IND-I_INDEPENDENT` or explicitly justified `IND-Q_QUALIFIED`;
- materially non-generic content;
- no target import explaining the match;
- explicit provenance and assumptions;
- appropriate physical bridge for physical claims.

Method 0.2.0 does not create a scalar `STRONG/MODERATE/WEAK` score. Historical terms remain historical.

## 7. Viability

Controlled recovery and physical realization can constitute positive viability evidence even when nonindependent.

Required statement:
- what the framework successfully realizes;
- whether the target was supplied;
- model/framework scope;
- robustness;
- missing realization/calibration burdens.

## 8. Genericity

The weaker-framework test remains mandatory where relevant, but its consequence is a tag, not deletion of the underlying result.

`MATHEMATICALLY_GENERIC` may reduce differential foundational significance without implying `EVIDENTIALLY_UNINFORMATIVE` for every question.

## 9. Empirical selection

Framework-specific empirical selection requires:
- comparator;
- compulsory/forced prediction or well-defined framework-conditioned prediction;
- free/fitted parameters;
- uncertainty model;
- decision rule;
- provenance;
- weaker-framework control;
- data relationship.

Recovered success of the target theory is `EMPIRICALLY_INHERITED` unless a separate discriminator exists.

## 10. Negative and unresolved states

Distinguish:

- `NONE_ESTABLISHED` — examined corpus supports no qualifying relation at declared scope.
- `UNRESOLVED_UNDER_FROZEN_CORPUS` — evidence is insufficient/ambiguous/inaccessible within the frozen window.
- `COUNTERMODELED` — explicit lawful countermodel defeats a stronger implication.
- `EMP-NEG_DIRECT_TENSION` — observational evidence directly pressures a claim at declared scope.

None of these is automatically framework-wide falsification.

## 11. Precommitment

Before framework-by-framework final classification:
- freeze source window;
- freeze comparator roles;
- freeze relation thresholds;
- freeze independence definitions;
- freeze viability and empirical consequence rules;
- declare known model/target/lineage dependencies.

Threshold changes after outcome inspection require a new method version and rerun plan.

## 12. Required comparison record

Every material relation record includes:
- framework IDs;
- comparator role(s);
- source IDs;
- K-key(s);
- historical/current selection status;
- scope level;
- relation type/subtype;
- evidence strength;
- provenance status + transcription flag;
- genericity tags;
- lineage status;
- target-conditioning;
- physical-realization;
- calibration;
- viability;
- independence;
- empirical status;
- weaker-framework test;
- overclaim test;
- over-subtraction test;
- known counterexamples/open burdens.

## 13. Permanent anti-smuggling controls

Retained:
- vocabulary/diagram/metaphor similarity is insufficient;
- generic mathematics is not framework-specific evidence;
- allowed process is not dynamics;
- mathematical structure is not physical structure without bridge;
- target recovery is not independent discovery;
- fitted input is not prediction;
- null incompleteness is not positive evidence;
- failure to solve a null open problem is not negative evidence unless solving it is part of the explicit framework claim/burden.

## 14. No automatic historical reinterpretation

If integrated, this file is prospective. Historical E1–E5 and convergence labels continue to mean what 0.1.0 defined when those artifacts were created.
