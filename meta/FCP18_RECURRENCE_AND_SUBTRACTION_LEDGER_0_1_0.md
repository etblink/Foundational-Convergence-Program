## 0. Governing question

FCP-18 asks whether any materially non-generic common structure recurs independently across multiple distinct framework families after the same controls that governed the original comparisons.

The required answer is:

`DOES_ANY_INDEPENDENTLY_NONGENERIC_COMMON_STRUCTURE_SURVIVE_ACROSS_MULTIPLE_DISTINCT_FRAMEWORK_FAMILIES = NO_AT_CURRENT_CLOSED_CORPUS`.

This is a bounded result through FCP-17, not a theorem that future frameworks cannot exhibit such recurrence.

## 2. Generic-mathematics subtraction

Generic subtraction removes or downgrades repeated use of:

- carriers as a generic requirement of formal modeling;
- equivalence relations and quotients;
- categories, composition and typed maps;
- operator/Hilbert/representation machinery;
- graph/network syntax;
- generic action, constraint and path-integral machinery;
- convexity/probability syntax absent framework-specific physical structure;
- formal observable/test families;
- generic adjacency/locality language;
- generic refinement/coarse-graining language;
- local-to-global/gluing/universal-property machinery.

This subtraction materially affects FCP-3, FCP-6, FCP-11, FCP-12, FCP-13, FCP-14, FCP-16 and FCP-17, and also functions as a control inside FCP-5.

After generic subtraction, substantial framework-specific residues remain individually:

- AQFT: physically specialized local-net/LCQFT organization, localized measurement, relative-Cauchy-response and named pAQFT extensions;
- GPTOPT: convex/operational state-effect geometry, composite-theory selection, broader-than-quantum theory space and bounded empirical narrowing;
- CST: locally finite causal-order/discreteness hypothesis, manifold-selection burden, optional dynamics and target-conditioned continuum bridges;
- CQM: process-first categorical quantum organization and named optional dagger/compact/classical/CPM structures;
- LOOP: nonperturbative quantum geometry, canonical/covariant dynamics programs, internal bridge, continuum/coarse-graining program and Barbero–Immirzi burden;
- Reduced NFC: source-bound `K_red=(C,T)`, conditional FIS/capacity results and the six carried survivor questions.

The existence of these individual residues demonstrates that generic subtraction is not merely deleting all non-null mathematics. It leaves substantive differences.

## 3. Lineage/reformulation subtraction

Lineage subtraction removes expected agreement from the independent-recurrence ledger:

### AQFT / QFT

FCP-5 permits strong bounded E2 representation relations while classifying core agreement as reformulation/structural sharpening within the QFT lineage. The shared empirical success is inherited through concrete QFT models.

### CQM / ordinary quantum theory

FCP-13 permits six bounded E2 relations while classifying them as process/representation reformulation or refinement of ordinary quantum theory. These E2 relations therefore do not count as independent recurrence.

### LOOP / GR

FCP-16 subtracts classical GR connection/constraint/diffeomorphism/Regge/Plebanski target lineage before retaining loop-specific quantum structure. Merely quantizing a GR-related arena is not independent rediscovery of GR.

### CQM / GPTOPT common quantum target

FCP-14 subtracts shared quantum-target content and bridge dependence. A common quantum realization is not an independent common foundational primitive.

After lineage subtraction, no multi-family E1–E4 recurrence remains qualified as independent.

## 4. Target-conditioned-recovery subtraction

Target conditioning is most visible in the quantum-gravity controls.

### CST / GR — FCP-11

Faithful embedding and selected operator/action/geometric continuum relations support real bounded E2/E3 subrelations. They remain target-conditioned because the Lorentzian/manifoldlike target or corresponding continuum structure is supplied or explicitly sought. They do not count as independent emergence of GR.

### LOOP / GR — FCP-16

Large-spin Regge asymptotics, low-order metric correlations and continuum/coarse-graining structures are scientifically substantive. Their GR target is explicit. FCP-16 also withholds E3 because the closed packet lacks the complete map/error/calibration record required by FCP-2.

### Reduced-NFC comparisons

FCP-12 and FCP-17 do not gain convergence merely because the comparator possesses a continuum or realization program. Reduced NFC has no source-bound pairwise controlled limit into those structures.

After target-conditioning subtraction, no independent multi-family strong or moderate convergence is qualified.

## 5. Empirical-inheritance subtraction

Empirical inheritance removes agreement that comes from reproducing an already successful comparator rather than independently predicting a discriminator.

Examples include:

- AQFT and CQM reproducing ordinary QFT/quantum empirical success in concrete models;
- CST continuum/GR-target success inheriting GR evidence rather than independently selecting CST;
- LOOP recovered-GR success inheriting GR evidence rather than independently selecting LOOP;
- Reduced-NFC comparisons gaining no credit from the comparator's empirical success.

FCP-14 additionally prevents GPTOPT's bounded empirical narrowing from being treated as evidence selecting CQM.

After empirical-inheritance subtraction:

`INDEPENDENT_FRAMEWORK_LEVEL_E4_IN_PAIRWISE_CORPUS = 0`.

## 8. Negative-result integrity

The absence of a shared common core is not an undifferentiated null result. The program distinguishes:

- **additional foundational structure:** CST causal/discrete carrier; LOOP quantum geometry/dynamics residue;
- **reformulation/lineage:** AQFT/QFT and CQM/QM strong bounded representations;
- **generic mathematics:** quotient/composition/graph/local-global shells across many comparisons;
- **target-conditioned recovery:** CST and LOOP continuum/GR relations;
- **model/extension structure:** AQFT extensions, GPTOPT reconstructions/composites, CST optional dynamics/phenomenology, CQM optional packages, LOOP formulation/model dependence;
- **physical-realization asymmetry:** especially Reduced NFC against null, AQFT, CST and LOOP;
- **empirical inheritance:** ordinary QFT/QM/GR success reproduced by related/targeted frameworks;
- **genuinely open questions:** dynamics selection, continuum selection, calibration, framework-level empirical discriminators, and some Reduced-NFC survivor questions.

This categorical separation is the main scientific information produced by the negative convergence record.
