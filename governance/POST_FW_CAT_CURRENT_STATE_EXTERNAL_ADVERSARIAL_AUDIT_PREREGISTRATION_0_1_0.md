Style preferences, filename preferences, ordinary documentation suggestions, or repository-organization opinions are not scientific findings unless they materially change interpretation, reproducibility, or authority resolution.

## Required finding schema

The frozen prompt must require, for each finding:

1. stable finding ID;
2. short title;
3. severity;
4. scientific materiality;
5. confidence;
6. exact affected artifact/claim/result;
7. exact packet evidence;
8. defect explanation;
9. direction of bias if applicable (`TOO_PERMISSIVE`, `TOO_STRICT`, `AMBIGUOUS`, `NONE`);
10. strongest justified consequence;
11. minimal remediation or re-test needed;
12. whether the issue changes science, method/governance only, provenance only, or documentation only.

Unsupported speculation must be labeled as such and may not be presented as a finding.
