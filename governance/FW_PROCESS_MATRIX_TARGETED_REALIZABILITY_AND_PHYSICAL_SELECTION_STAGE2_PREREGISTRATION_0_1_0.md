A result may be classified as a `GENERAL_FRAMEWORK_WIDE_PHYSICAL_SELECTION_CRITERION` only if all of the following hold:


## 21. Adversarial invariance tests before freeze acceptance

Before this preregistration is accepted, Project Lead review must verify:

1. **Universal-positive control:** a hypothetical frozen corpus containing a genuine necessary-and-sufficient framework-wide physical criterion could yield AX1=`ESTABLISHED` and A=`SUPPORTED`.
2. **Fragmented-positive control:** a corpus containing several real but assumption-dependent subclasses and no general criterion can yield B/C without being mislabeled a framework failure.
3. **Adverse-boundary control:** broad source-bound no-go theorems can support D without being inflated into whole-framework impossibility.
4. **Mixed control:** positive subclasses plus no-go boundaries plus an unresolved remainder can yield B/C/D/E simultaneously.
5. **Null-strengthening control:** if all 11 new sources were proposition-redundant, F could be supported.
6. **Representation control:** a proof that every `W` has a probabilistic pre/postselected representation can establish AX3 while leaving AX1 and AX2 unforced.
7. **Framework-neutrality control:** no criterion depends on the observed title, author, valence, or desired survival of `FW-PROCESS-MATRIX`.

Failure of any invariance test requires preregistration repair **before** Stage-2 corpus application.
