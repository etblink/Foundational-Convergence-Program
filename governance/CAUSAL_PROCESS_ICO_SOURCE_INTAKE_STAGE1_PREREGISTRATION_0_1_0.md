## 7. Frozen discovery lanes

Every lane D1–D13 must be searched. Each ends as `PASS`, `NO_QUALIFYING_SOURCE_FOUND_AT_DECLARED_SCOPE`, or `FAIL`.

### D1 — Process-matrix formalism and generalized process class
Origins, definitions, primitive inputs/outputs, local-laboratory assumptions, admissible generalized processes, normalization/consistency structure, physical scope, and explicit limitations.

### D2 — Indefinite causal order as property, resource, or correlation class
Distinguish indefinite causal order, causal nonseparability, coherent control of order, causal indefiniteness, and related resource/property notions rather than assuming equivalence.

### D3 — Quantum switch and higher-order process models
Quantum-switch constructions, controlled order, higher-order process models, and the boundary between particular switch families and the broader process-matrix/ICO landscape.

### D4 — Quantum combs, supermaps, process tensors, and higher-order transformations
Higher-order transformations and adjacent process formalisms where they materially inform whether higher-order structure is broader, narrower, equivalent to, or merely adjacent to ICO notions.

### D5 — Causal nonseparability, witnesses, and resource structure
Definitions, witnesses, resource structure, conversion/order relations where available, and limitations on treating causal nonseparability as framework identity.

### D6 — Causal inequalities and device-independent constraints
Causal inequalities, correlations incompatible with definite causal order, device-independent/device-dependent distinctions, and limitations connecting inequality violation to causal nonseparability, process matrices, or physical ICO.

### D7 — Operational / GPT / reconstruction relations
Operational and generalized-probabilistic formulations, reconstruction principles, probabilistic process structures, and explicit source boundaries relative to `FW-GPTOPT`.

### D8 — Categorical / process-theoretic semantics
Categorical semantics, process-theory encodings, causal categories, higher-order categorical structure, and source boundaries relative to `FW-CQM` and historical `FW-CAT`.

### D9 — Physical realizability, no-go results, and implementation constraints
Physical-realizability restrictions; postselection, dilation/purification, local-laboratory or time-delocalized assumptions where directly relevant; simulation versus realization; definite-order explanations; and no-go/limitation results.

### D10 — Composition, time, evolution, and dynamical-architecture characterization
What sources treat as composition, transformation, causal structure, time, evolution, or physical-law selection. This lane is explicitly **not** a criterion-C rescue search and receives no privileged depth.

### D11 — Laboratory, experimental, simulator, and empirical scope
Physical implementations, witnesses, controlled experiments, simulator/analogue realizations, and the exact evidential ceiling of those results.

### D12 — Quantum-gravity-motivated causal structure and embedding boundary
Direct causal-process/ICO links to quantum-gravity motivation, plus embedding gaps and distinctions from mature quantum-gravity framework status. General QG material without direct relevance is excluded.

### D13 — Foundational interpretation, taxonomy, critique, history, and synthesis
Authoritative reviews, historical/scope syntheses, foundational interpretations, taxonomy discussions, criticism, heterogeneity, and framework-identity cautions.

The lane set is frozen before search and may not be reweighted, renamed, dropped, or expanded because early results look promising or disappointing.

## 22. Coverage, saturation, and stop rule

There is no target source count. Discovery may stop only after:

1. all D1–D13 lanes have been searched;
2. all three passes are complete;
3. every serious subfamily has adequate identity/core coverage or an explicit bounded no-source result;
4. CQM representation overlap and identity coverage are separately audited;
5. GPTOPT operational overlap and identity coverage are separately audited;
6. higher-order-process, process-matrix, and ICO specificity are separately tracked;
7. every serious candidate family receives an adverse/limitation search;
8. model/implementation evidence is separated from framework-level claims;
9. QG-motivated material has an explicit embedding/limitation boundary;
10. source identities and duplicate versions are resolved;
11. meaningful rejections and deferrals are preserved;
12. TQ1–TQ12 coverage is audited;
13. repeated frozen queries yield predominantly duplicate, lower-authority, redundant, or out-of-scope material; and
14. no known unresolved source gap would predictably change whether a later taxonomy adjudication is responsible.

Saturation is bounded to the declared surfaces, query families, cutoff, and passes. It never means universal literature completeness.
