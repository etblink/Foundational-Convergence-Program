## 7. Mandatory search lanes

Every lane must be searched adversarially. A lane without an admissible source must be reported as an explicit coverage gap.

### Lane A — tensor-network state architecture

Search matrix-product states, the DMRG–MPS relation, PEPS, general tensor-network states, bond dimension, entanglement structure, area-law relations, and exact versus approximate representation. The purpose is to identify what is represented without promoting representation to ontology.

### Lane B — entanglement renormalization and MERA

Search entanglement renormalization, MERA, scale structure, real-space RG, causal cones, network geometry, and critical-system representation. Separate representational and RG machinery from spacetime, causality, or foundational claims.

### Lane C — tensor-network renormalization and dynamics

Search sources distinguishing renormalization, coarse graining, variational evolution, real- and imaginary-time evolution, optimization, and physical dynamics.

### Lane D — entanglement/information and emergent geometry

Search primary and critical sources connecting entanglement with connectivity, area, distance, geometry, spacetime emergence, gravity, or Einstein equations. Record all supplied targets, limits, model classes, and scope ceilings.

### Lane E — holographic tensor networks

Search MERA/AdS analogies, holographic and perfect-tensor networks, random tensor networks, RT-like entropy relations, bulk–boundary reconstruction, and entanglement-wedge reconstruction. Distinguish AdS/CFT or holographic-lineage content from independent tensor-network architecture.

### Lane F — quantum error correction and information-theoretic reconstruction

Search QEC, operator-algebra QEC, bulk reconstruction, subregion duality, redundancy, encoding, and information recovery. Index QEC as mathematical description, analogy, duality dictionary, physical mechanism, or claimed foundational primitive without adjudicating which reading survives.

### Lane G — explicitly information-first foundational proposals

Search credible programs that assign information, entanglement, code structure, network structure, or related objects a direct foundational physical role. Generic quantum information, GPT, CQM, quantum computing, and information geometry are excluded absent a direct claim relevant to the historical umbrella.

### Lane H — limitations, counterexamples, and critical sources

Actively search representational nonuniqueness, gauge redundancy, network dependence, bond-dimension and approximation limits, model and background dependence, AdS specificity, failures of universal geometric interpretation, absence of physical dynamics, absence of calibrated observables, and lack of empirical discrimination.

### Lane I — physical realization, observables, phenomenology, and empirical contact

Search physical realization, observable construction, calibration, low-energy recovery, gravitational/QFT/condensed-matter predictions, experimental or observational contact, and falsification. A null framework-level result is acceptable. Simulation of a supplied theory is not framework-level empirical evidence.
