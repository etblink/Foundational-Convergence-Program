## 5. External-source discovery purpose

External literature is admitted only to answer one or more of these questions:

1. Is the mathematical `G` / `T_ind` architecture standard triangle-network / network-local / causal-model mathematics rather than a PGH-specific formal object?
2. What exactly follows mathematically from independent latent sources and local response kernels?
3. Which observed distributions are known to lie outside the relevant triangle/network-local class, and by what proof/relaxation methods?
4. Does any external literature supply a physical interpretation that PGH is silently relying on?
5. Is PGH's universal physical claim already an existing framework/model postulate under another established name?
6. Which FCP framework identities are the strongest plausible hosts/competitors for criterion-G testing?
7. What limitations, representation dependence, latent-variable ambiguities, or causal-compatibility caveats bear directly on PGH's claimed physical identity?

External source existence is not evidence that PGH is true.

## 8. Source hierarchy and admission rules

Preferred order:

1. primary research papers / original theorem sources;
2. authoritative technical reviews only where they add taxonomy/history synthesis not efficiently recoverable from primary papers;
3. canonical PGH repository artifacts for proposal identity/provenance;
4. canonical FCP framework records for existing-framework identity boundaries.

Do not admit:

- news coverage;
- generic encyclopedia summaries;
- duplicated secondary restatements;
- papers mentioning triangle networks without bearing on the candidate identity;
- PGH self-assessment as independent validation;
- sources selected because they make PGH look favorable or unfavorable without satisfying a frozen lane.
