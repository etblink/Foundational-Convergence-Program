## 11. Severity ladder

```text
CRITICAL
HIGH
MEDIUM
LOW
INFORMATIONAL
```

Definitions:

- `CRITICAL`: if true, invalidates or materially corrupts a large fraction of current program results or method.
- `HIGH`: if true, materially changes a framework, method rule, major FCP-22–FCP-24 result, or recurrence readiness.
- `MEDIUM`: if true, requires bounded reanalysis, source correction, taxonomy refinement, or governance repair.
- `LOW`: local defect with limited scientific impact.
- `INFORMATIONAL`: useful caution or improvement without current result change.

Every finding must identify one primary domain from `METHOD`, `TAXONOMY`, `SOURCE_SELECTION`, `SOURCE_PROVENANCE`, `RELATION_EVIDENCE`, `EMPIRICAL_BURDEN`, `REALIZATION`, `RECURRENCE`, `GOVERNANCE`, `DOCUMENTATION`, or `OTHER`; secondary domains may also be listed.
