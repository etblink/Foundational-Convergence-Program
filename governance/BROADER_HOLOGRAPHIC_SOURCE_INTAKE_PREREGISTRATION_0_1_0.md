## 5. Mandatory search strata

The first-pass search must deliberately cover all of the following strata:

1. **Foundational holographic principle and entropy-bound lineage:** black-hole entropy, area scaling, information bounds, the holographic principle, and the covariant entropy bound.
2. **AdS/CFT and gauge/gravity duality:** original formulations, the gauge/gravity dictionary, bulk–boundary correspondence, large-`N` and strong-coupling limits, string/M embedding, and bulk locality.
3. **Bulk reconstruction:** HKLL or related reconstruction, entanglement-wedge reconstruction, subregion duality, bulk-operator encoding, and reconstruction limitations.
4. **Entanglement and emergent geometry:** RT/HRT, generalized entropy, entanglement–geometry relations, the first law of entanglement, Einstein equations from entanglement, and connectivity.
5. **Quantum error correction and holographic codes:** AdS/CFT as QEC, operator-algebra QEC, holographic codes, subsystem encoding, and entanglement-wedge QEC.
6. **Tensor-network relations to holography:** only sources needed to distinguish models, codes, geometric representations, AdS/CFT structure, and broader holographic claims; relevant FCP-25 sources may be reused without duplicate register rows.
7. **Black-hole information and modern holographic unitarity:** Page curve, islands, replica wormholes, generalized entropy, entanglement wedge, and unitarity in holographic settings.
8. **Cosmological and de Sitter holography:** dS/CFT, cosmological holography, wavefunction/boundary relations, dS entropy, static-patch proposals, and constructive as well as limiting literature.
9. **Flat-space and celestial holography:** BMS symmetry, celestial CFT, amplitude/boundary dictionaries, and flat-space holographic proposals.
10. **Broader non-AdS or generalized programs:** high-value taxonomy sources encountered for matrix-model, nonrelativistic, AdS/QCD, higher-spin, SYK/JT, lower-dimensional, or quantum-information reconstruction programs.

The tenth stratum is discovery-driven: a source is admitted only when it materially informs the later taxonomy question.

## 6. Search method and two-pass rule

Search surfaces may include authoritative journal and publisher records, DOI metadata, arXiv records and full text, author or institutional pages, technical reviews, and bibliographic trails in admitted primary sources. Search-engine results are discovery aids only.

```text
FIRST_PASS = BROAD_FAMILY_DISCOVERY
SECOND_PASS = GAP_TARGETED_SEARCH
ARBITRARY_SOURCE_QUOTA = NONE
COVERAGE_DRIVEN_SELECTION = YES
```

The first pass will identify seminal constructions, later load-bearing technical results, limitations, and authoritative syntheses across every mandatory stratum. A coverage matrix will then expose weak categories. The second pass will target those gaps, including adverse or limiting literature, underrepresented non-AdS programs, and unresolved source identities.

Search may stop only when further candidates are primarily redundant, lower authority, or out of scope rather than introducing a new load-bearing category. Any saturation statement is bounded to the declared search scope and does not claim universal literature completeness.

## 7. Source-quality hierarchy

Preference order:

```text
1 = SEMINAL_PRIMARY_RESEARCH_PAPER
2 = PRIMARY_TECHNICAL_FOLLOWUP
3 = AUTHORITATIVE_TECHNICAL_REVIEW_OR_LECTURE_NOTES
4 = MAJOR_SYNTHESIS_REVIEW
5 = EXPERIMENTAL_OR_SIMULATION_PRIMARY_SOURCE_WHERE_RELEVANT
```

Lower-tier material may aid terminology, historical linkage, or discovery but may not carry a load-bearing scientific role when an adequate primary source is available. Blogs, popular expositions, search snippets, and generic summaries are not admissible scientific authority.

## 8. Admission rules

A discovered candidate may be admitted only when its relevance is direct, its authority is sufficient for the assigned role, and redundancy is low or specifically justified. It must satisfy at least one of:

```text
A = DEFINES_OR_ORIGINATES_A_MAJOR_HOLOGRAPHIC_PRINCIPLE_OR_DUALITY
B = ESTABLISHES_A_LOAD_BEARING_TECHNICAL_RESULT_NEEDED_FOR_LATER_TAXONOMY
C = CLARIFIES_PRIMITIVES_DYNAMICS_DICTIONARY_OR_RECONSTRUCTION_SCOPE
D = ESTABLISHES_A_MAJOR_LIMITATION_AMBIGUITY_OR_NO_GO_RELEVANT_TO_TAXONOMY
E = ESTABLISHES_REALIZATION_EMPIRICAL_OR_SIMULATION_SCOPE_RELEVANT_TO_LATER_ADJUDICATION
F = AUTHORITATIVE_REVIEW_REQUIRED_TO_SYNTHESIZE_FRAGMENTED_PRIMARY_LITERATURE
```

For every admitted source, the intake will record exact bibliographic identity, stratum, intake role, admission reason, later taxonomy question supported, known scope or limitation, new/reused status, and FCP-24/FCP-25 overlap tags.

## 9. Rejection rules

A candidate will be rejected when it is purely pedagogical despite an available primary source, redundant without new taxonomy value, speculative without sufficient technical content, a blog or popular exposition, a secondary summary without load-bearing need, outside the later taxonomy question, solely tensor-network material already covered by FCP-25, or general quantum-gravity material without a holographic role.

Meaningful rejections will be retained in the source-selection audit to expose selection discipline. Rejected sources receive no Source Register row.

```text
DISCOVERED_CANDIDATE_SOURCE != ADMITTED_FROZEN_SOURCE
REJECTION_BECAUSE_SOURCE_WEAKENS_A_STRONG_HOLOGRAPHIC_READING = FORBIDDEN
ADMISSION_BECAUSE_SOURCE_SUPPORTS_A_DESIRED_TAXONOMY = FORBIDDEN
```
