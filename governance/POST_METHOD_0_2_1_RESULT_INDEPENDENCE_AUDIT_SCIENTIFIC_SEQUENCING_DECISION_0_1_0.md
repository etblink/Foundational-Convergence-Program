The controlling ambiguity from R1 is:

> Must a reformulation/meta-framework satisfy criterion C through independently native physical law/constraint content, or may it source-bind the law architecture of a theory it reformulates after generic representational mathematics is subtracted?

`FW-PROCESS-MATRIX` is described by Stage 2 as a `SOURCE_BOUND_GENERALIZING_CAUSAL_PROCESS_META_FRAMEWORK`. That label makes the ambiguity potentially relevant to framework-category semantics.

However, the Stage-2 C pass for `FW-PROCESS-MATRIX` is not claimed by inheriting ordinary quantum dynamics, CQM categorical structure, or generic GPTOPT admissibility. It is claimed from process-matrix-specific positivity, normalization, consistency, causal-correlation, composition-validity and realizability constraints on a global process object linked to local interventions.

Stage 2 also separately finds that neither `FW-CQM` nor `FW-GPTOPT` identity-covers the full object.
