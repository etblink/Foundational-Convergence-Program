## 6. Search scope and required lanes

The search will cover the following thirteen lanes where qualifying literature exists:

1. historical and current identity of category-theoretic foundational programs;
2. the CQM and quantum-process-theory boundary;
3. general process-theoretic or compositional foundations beyond CQM;
4. topos approaches to quantum theory and physics;
5. categorical probability, Markov categories, or probabilistic process structures only where a foundational-physics claim is made;
6. categorical or functorial QFT;
7. TQFT, cobordism, and higher-categorical physics, separating classification or representation from foundational physical commitment;
8. category-theoretic or higher-categorical quantum-gravity candidates without presuming unity;
9. dynamics, time, causality, measurement, and composition;
10. realization, reconstruction, low-energy, or operational bridges;
11. empirical, phenomenological, or experimental status, including a qualified absence finding;
12. limitations, criticism, negative results, and taxonomy boundaries;
13. current specialist reviews or synthesis sources.

Search vocabulary may include categorical quantum mechanics, quantum process theories, process theories, operational process theories, compositional foundations, effectus theory, operational effect theories, topos quantum theory, neo-realist topos approaches, categorical probability, Markov categories, categorical QFT, functorial QFT, algebraic QFT functors, TQFT, cobordism hypothesis, higher categories in physics, higher gauge theory, categorical quantum gravity, dynamics in process theories, causality in process theories, categorical measurement, physical realization, empirical status, critique, limitation, scope, and taxonomy.

## 8. Source authority and text sufficiency

Preference order is:

1. primary peer-reviewed research establishing the relevant scientific claim;
2. authoritative specialist monographs or mature reviews for taxonomy, history, or current synthesis;
3. high-quality primary preprints when no final form adequately supplies the result;
4. official proceedings or scholarly chapters when they are the appropriate primary venue.

Wikipedia, general encyclopedias, popular articles, blogs, unsourced webpages, AI-generated summaries, and search-result snippets are not admissible scientific authority.

An abstract may establish identity, topic, and broad claim direction. It may not by itself establish subtle propositions concerning primitives, framework equivalence, taxonomy, dynamics, realization, no-go results, limitations, or empirical scope when qualifications occur in the body.

```text
IDENTITY_RESOLVED = REQUIRED_FOR_ADMISSION
BIBLIOGRAPHIC_METADATA_VERIFIED = REQUIRED_FOR_ADMISSION
```

Third-party source files will not be committed. The frozen corpus consists only of bibliographic bindings and concise role, proposition, scope, limitation, reuse, and access metadata.

## 13. Coverage and saturation rule

Discovery may stop only when:

1. every required lane has been searched;
2. every serious discovered subfamily has adequate identity and core coverage;
3. the `FW-CQM` and `FW-GPTOPT` boundaries are represented;
4. at least one meaningful limitation, negative, or boundary source has been sought for each serious candidate family where such literature exists;
5. repeated searches yield predominantly duplicate, lower-authority, or propositionally redundant material; and
6. no known unresolved source gap would predictably change a later taxonomy verdict.

Each lane will end as `PASS`, `NO_QUALIFYING_SOURCE`, or `FAIL`. Saturation is bounded to the declared search scope and does not assert universal completeness.
